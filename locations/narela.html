practical guidance

renovating an older home in narela

Check the structure first

Older houses often need beams, slabs or columns strengthened before finishes. We have this checked before the design is fixed.

Plan the facade with the plan

A new elevation is easier and cheaper when it is drawn with the interior layout, not after it.

Fix the budget before finishes

We agree a budget range at the alignment stage and develop an itemised BOQ, so material choices are made against real numbers.

questions, answered.

Where is Indéva Studio’s office in Narela?

Our studio and site office is at 34/11, Lampur Road, Industrial Area, Narela, Delhi 110040. Visits are by appointment.

What kind of projects do you take on in Narela?

Independent houses and villas, DDA flats, builder floors, shops and offices — design only, or design with execution.

Do you have completed work in Narela?

Yes. The Villa Project, Narela (a 35-year-old house with a new Dholpur stone facade) is ongoing, and our own studio office in Narela is published as a case study.

How much does interior design cost in Narela?

It depends on area, scope and finish level. We discuss budget openly before design starts and prepare an itemised BOQ rather than quoting a flat rate.

Can I see materials before choosing them?

Yes. Our Narela studio keeps material samples on display, and we review finishes with you there before they are ordered.
