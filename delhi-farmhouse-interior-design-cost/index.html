the surprising truth about interior design costs in india

when it comes to interior design, many of us believe that hiring a professional will automatically break the bank. however, with a clear understanding of the costs involved, you can create your dream space without overspending. for instance, a duplex in Greater Kailash, Delhi with a budget of ₹40–80 lakh can be designed to perfection with the right guidance. at indéva studio, we've worked with numerous clients to bring their luxury farmhouse visions to life, and we're here to share our expertise with you.

what drives costs up in interior design projects

several factors contribute to the overall cost of an interior design project. these include the size of the space, the materials and finishes chosen, and the complexity of the design. for a duplex in Delhi, you can expect to pay anywhere from ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 per square foot, depending on the level of luxury and customization you desire. to give you a better idea, here's a rough breakdown of what you might expect to pay for different aspects of your design project:

  • flooring: ₹500 to ₹2,000 per square foot
  • walls and ceilings: ₹800 to ₹3,500 per square foot
  • lighting: ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per fixture
  • furniture: ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000 per piece

cost breakdown by room

to help you plan your budget more effectively, let's take a look at the typical costs associated with different rooms in a duplex. for example:

living room

a luxury living room in a Delhi farmhouse might cost anywhere from ₹5 to ₹15 lakh to design and furnish, depending on the materials and furniture you choose. this could include:

  • sofa: ₹1 to ₹3 lakh
  • armchairs: ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh each
  • coffee table: ₹20,000 to ₹50,000

kitchen

a high-end kitchen in a duplex might cost anywhere from ₹8 to ₹20 lakh to design and equip, depending on the appliances, materials, and layout you choose. this could include:

  • cabinets: ₹2 to ₹5 lakh
  • countertops: ₹1 to ₹3 lakh
  • appliances: ₹1 to ₹2 lakh

how to negotiate with your interior designer

when working with an interior designer, it's essential to communicate your budget and expectations clearly. be sure to ask about any potential costs or fees associated with the project, and don't be afraid to negotiate. as elle decor india suggests, a good designer will work with you to create a space that meets your needs and fits your budget. at indéva studio, we're committed to transparency and open communication throughout the design process. you can learn more about our design process and how we work with clients to bring their visions to life.

the key to successful negotiation is to prioritize your needs and be flexible. consider what aspects of the design are most important to you, and be willing to compromise on others. this will help you stay within your budget of ₹40–80 lakh while still achieving the luxury look and feel you desire.

red flags that inflate bills

there are several red flags to watch out for when working with an interior designer to avoid inflated bills. these include:

  • unclear or vague contracts
  • unexpected changes or additions to the design plan
  • lack of transparency regarding costs or materials

to avoid these issues, be sure to carefully review your contract and ask questions if you're unsure about anything. you can also browse our portfolio to see examples of our previous work and get a sense of our design style and approach.
