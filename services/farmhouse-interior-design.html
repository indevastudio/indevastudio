Farmhouse interior design

Large-volume family residences where generous space is organised into homes that feel calm and connected — planned from structure, built to drawings.

The challenge in a farmhouse is scale. Big, open volumes need to be organised into distinct zones for living, dining, bedrooms and guests without breaking the sightlines that make the space special.

We plan farmhouse interiors from the structure up, design the furniture to suit the proportions of the rooms, and execute on site with our own team — as on our 4,800 sq ft Mini Farmhouse in Delhi.

What’s included

  • Zoning of large, open volumes
  • Living, dining and family lounges
  • Bedroom suites and bathrooms
  • Kitchens, pantries and utility areas
  • Lighting for double-height and large spaces
  • Custom furniture scaled to the rooms
  • Indoor–outdoor transitions and verandahs
  • Itemised BOQ and site execution

How we work

  1. Site visit and briefWe measure the space, understand how the household or business will use it, and agree scope, timeline and budget.
  2. Space planningLayouts, zoning and circulation are fixed first — the plan is settled before any finish is chosen.
  3. Design and 3D viewsMaterials, lighting and furniture are developed with 3D visualisation so you see the space before it is built.
  4. Drawings and BOQWorking drawings and an itemised bill of quantities — every item with its material, brand and rate — so costs are clear before work starts.
  5. Execution and supervisionOur team coordinates vendors and supervises the site so the work is built to the drawings.
  6. HandoverFinal checks, snag correction and a clean handover of the finished space.
