Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect personal data. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Who we are

Indéva Studio (“we”, “us”) is an interior design and construction studio at 34/11, Lampur Road, Industrial Area, Narela, Delhi 110040. This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through indevastudio.com and the services we provide.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you fill in a form, call, email or message us on WhatsApp: your name, phone number, email address, project location, property type, area, budget, timeline and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • Client accounts. If you are a client with access to a project statement or estimate: your email address and login details, the project information we share with you, your approvals, and a record of each sign-in and PDF download, including the date and time, device and browser, IP address and the approximate location (city, area and postal code) derived from that IP address.
  • Website use. Pages visited, device and browser type, referring site and similar information collected through cookies and Google Analytics, where you allow them through the cookie banner.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals and estimates, and deliver your project.
  • To keep client accounts and project statements secure, including detecting unusual or unauthorised sign-ins.
  • To send you information about your project and, where you have asked for it or we have a legitimate reason, occasional follow-ups about our services. You can opt out at any time.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and our work, and only as needed: website hosting (Vercel), database and client login (Supabase), email delivery (EmailJS and Brevo), website analytics (Google Analytics) and cookie consent (Cookiebot). We may also share project details with our own team, contractors and vendors working on your project, and with authorities where the law requires it. Some of these providers store data outside India.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as they are useful for following up, normally up to three years. Client project records, including sign-in records, are kept for the duration of the project and for as long afterwards as needed for warranty, accounting and legal purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct or update it, or to delete it where we are not required to keep it. You can also withdraw consent you have given, for example to marketing messages. Write to us at hello@indevastudio.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections and per-client access to project pages. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login details private and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used them.

Questions and complaints

For any question or complaint about how we handle your personal data, contact Arpit Saini, Founder & CEO, at hello@indevastudio.com or +91 97178 81083.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page. Last updated 5 November 2026.
