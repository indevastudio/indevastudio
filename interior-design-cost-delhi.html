What makes interiors cost more (or less)

  • Shutter finish. Laminate is the value choice; acrylic adds about 30% to a kitchen and PU nearly 60%.
  • Loose furniture. Sofa, dining and beds are often a quarter of the budget. Many quotes leave them out — check before comparing.
  • Rewiring. Working on the builder's conduits costs ₹100 per sq.ft.; rewiring everything costs ₹250.
  • Civil work and flooring. Our prices keep the builder's flooring, tiles and sanitaryware. Replacing floors or bathrooms is a separate budget.
  • Motorised curtains, chandeliers, wallpaper and feature walls are where budgets quietly grow — we list them as add-ons so you choose.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 2 BHK interior cost in Delhi?

Complete interiors for a 2 BHK of about 800 sq.ft. carpet area — ceiling, electrical, lighting, paint, curtains, modular kitchen, wardrobes and loose furniture — cost about ₹12 lakh plus GST in our Standard specification, and about 30% more in Premium.

What is the interior design cost per square foot in Delhi?

For complete interiors including furniture, plan on roughly ₹1,500–2,000 per sq.ft. of carpet area at a good mid-range specification. Modular kitchens and wardrobes are priced separately per sq.ft. of front: ₹1,400 to ₹2,200 depending on the finish.

How much does a 1 BHK interior cost in Delhi?

Our complete 1 BHK package for a 318 sq.ft. DDA flat comes to about ₹5.4 lakh plus GST, including a modular kitchen, wardrobe, bed, sofa and TV unit.

How much does a 3 BHK interior cost in Delhi?

For a 3 BHK of about 1,080 sq.ft. carpet area, complete interiors with furniture come to about ₹21 lakh plus GST in our Standard specification.

What is the modular kitchen cost in Delhi?

Our modular kitchen rate is ₹1,400 per sq.ft. of front in laminate, ₹1,800 in acrylic and ₹2,200 in PU. A typical 2 BHK kitchen of about 85 sq.ft. of front comes to about ₹1.2 lakh in laminate before hardware, countertop and sink.

Is GST included in these prices?

No. All prices on this page exclude GST, which is added to the invoice.
