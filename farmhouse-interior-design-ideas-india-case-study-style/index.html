farmhouse interior design ideas india: a case study

in a quiet neighborhood of south delhi, delhi, our client, a family of four, lived in an independent bungalow that lacked the warmth and character they desired. with a budget of ₹15–25 lakh, they approached indéva studio to give their home a luxurious makeover, incorporating farmhouse interior design ideas that would reflect their personality and style.

the brief

the client's brief was clear: they wanted a spacious, well-lit, and airy home that would exude a sense of luxury and comfort. they were particularly drawn to farmhouse interior design ideas, which they felt would add a touch of rustic charm to their modern bungalow. our team at indéva studio was tasked with bringing this vision to life, within the allocated budget of ₹15–25 lakh.

the challenge

one of the major challenges we faced was the limited budget of ₹15–25 lakh, which had to cover all aspects of the design, including materials, labor, and furniture. another challenge was the client's desire to incorporate traditional farmhouse elements, such as wooden beams and stone walls, into a modern bungalow. our team had to think creatively to balance these elements and stay within the budget.

design decisions

to address the challenges, we made several key design decisions. firstly, we opted for locally sourced materials, such as wooden furniture and natural stone, to reduce costs. we also decided to retain some of the existing features of the bungalow, such as the wooden flooring, to minimize waste and save on renovation costs. additionally, we incorporated plenty of greenery and natural light to create a sense of airiness and spaciousness.

  • locally sourced materials: ₹3 lakh
  • retained existing features: saved ₹2 lakh
  • incorporated greenery and natural light: ₹1.5 lakh

the design solution

our design solution was to create a harmonious blend of traditional and modern elements, while staying within the budget of ₹15–25 lakh. we achieved this by using a combination of natural materials, such as wood and stone, and modern furniture pieces. the result was a luxurious and comfortable home that reflected the client's personality and style.

the key to successful farmhouse interior design is to strike a balance between rustic and modern elements, creating a sense of harmony and cohesion. this can be achieved by using natural materials, such as wood and stone, and incorporating plenty of greenery and natural light.

the result

the final result was a stunning farmhouse interior design that exceeded the client's expectations. the total cost of the project came in at ₹20 lakh, which was within the allocated budget of ₹15–25 lakh. the client was thrilled with the outcome and praised our team at indéva studio for their creativity and attention to detail.
