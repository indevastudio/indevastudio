01

Introduction

The family called it the White House. Casa Bianca is a 10,000 sq. ft. private farmhouse near Sohna, built in a neoclassical style and planned around how a large family actually spends its weekends: together in one double-height hall, outdoors by the pool, and in rooms of their own.

Indéva Studio carried the project from planning and structure through waterproofing, the pool, interiors, the kitchen and services.

02

The client brief

  • Around ten bedrooms, each with an attached bathroom
  • A basement for recreation
  • An eight-shaped swimming pool with a granite deck
  • Landscaped gardens, wide balconies and a guard room at the gate
  • A plan that follows Vastu
  • A fixed budget, without compromising how long the house will last
03

Architectural concept

A Vastu-compliant layout with neoclassical detailing, generous bedrooms and planned natural ventilation. The house reads as a calm white mass on the lawn, with a tiled porch roof marking the entrance and deep overhangs shading the upper balconies from the summer sun.

04

Design language

Outside: white neoclassical facades, wrap-around balconies with black metal railings and tall windows on the garden side. Inside: cove-lit false ceilings, a tiered crystal chandelier at the heart of the living hall, carved and upholstered beds, and a restrained champagne, cream and walnut palette repeated room to room.

05

Space planning

A double-height living hall overlooked by a glass-railed gallery, with dining beside it and a kitchen with a breakfast counter and a separate pantry behind rolling shutters. Around ten bedroom suites sit across the floors, with the recreation area in the basement and the pool and lawns on the garden side.

06

Construction journey

Building at this scale on a remote farmhouse site meant working through temperatures of up to 45°C, frequent power cuts, scarce local supplies and costly transport.

The ground was the harder problem. High, saline groundwater made basement waterproofing, structural integrity, drainage slopes, balcony levels and plumbing all demand careful planning before anything was built.

07

Interior detailing

  • Ceilings: POP and false-ceiling detailing with concealed cove lighting in the hall and bedrooms.
  • Lighting: decorative lighting throughout and one statement chandelier in the living hall.
  • Bathrooms: marble-look walls, feature walls in herringbone wood and geometric tile, black vessel basins and walk-in glass showers.
  • Pool: granite coping custom-cut to follow the curves of the eight-shaped pool.
