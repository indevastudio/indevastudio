The vision

Envisioned by the family as the White House, Casa Bianca is a 10,000 sq. ft. farmhouse in a neoclassical style: an expansive private retreat combining architectural grandeur, contemporary comfort and planned spaces for recreation.

It has around ten bedrooms with attached bathrooms, a basement recreation area, an eight-shaped swimming pool with a granite deck, landscaped gardens, wide balconies and a guard room at the gate.

The challenge

Building at this scale on a remote farmhouse site meant working through temperatures of up to 45°C, frequent power cuts, scarce local supplies and costly transport.

The ground was the harder problem. High, saline groundwater made basement waterproofing, structural integrity, drainage slopes, balcony levels and plumbing all demand careful planning — within a fixed budget, without compromising quality or how long the house will last.

The Indéva approach

We gave architectural detailing, structural reliability and material selection equal attention.

  • Architecture & planning: A Vastu-compliant layout with neoclassical detailing, generous bedrooms and planned natural ventilation.
  • Structure & waterproofing: Waterproofing across the basement, bathrooms, balconies and terrace, designed against groundwater pressure and long-term moisture.
  • Swimming pool & landscape: An eight-shaped pool with custom-cut granite coping shaped to follow its curves, with a landscaped garden and outdoor seating.
  • Interiors & finishes: Premium flooring, UPVC windows, POP and false-ceiling detailing, decorative lighting and a statement chandelier at the heart of the living hall.
  • Kitchen: A laminate-finished HDHMR kitchen with a breakfast counter and a separate pantry behind rolling shutters.
  • MEP & engineering: Electrical, plumbing and drainage coordinated for dependable everyday performance.

The outcome

Casa Bianca brings neoclassical elegance and practical engineering together in a large private residence. From the precision-cut granite pool deck to the waterproofed basement, every element was considered for how it looks, how it works and how long it lasts.

The result is a farmhouse that feels grand without losing sight of comfort and the finer details of everyday living.

10,000 sq. ft. of considered design. One vision, executed down to the last detail.
