couple bedroom interior design delhi: a case study

in a bustling city like delhi, finding a peaceful retreat is a luxury. for one couple, this retreat was their bedroom in a builder floor in sector 44, noida. they approached indéva studio with a vision to create a serene and stylish space that reflected their personalities. with a budget of ₹15-25 lakh, we embarked on a design journey to bring their vision to life.

the brief

the client's brief was clear: they wanted a bedroom that exuded warmth, comfort, and elegance. they had a penchant for luxury and wanted their bedroom to reflect that. with a total area of 250 square feet, we had to ensure that every inch of the space was utilized efficiently. our team at indéva studio spent several hours discussing the project, understanding the client's preferences, and defining the scope of work.

the challenge

the biggest challenge we faced was the limited budget. with ₹15-25 lakh, we had to be mindful of every design decision, ensuring that it was both aesthetically pleasing and cost-effective. another challenge was the compact size of the bedroom, which meant that we had to be creative with the layout and furniture selection. we also had to consider the natural light and ventilation in the room, as it was a critical factor in creating a comfortable and healthy environment.

understanding the client's preferences

our team spent several hours with the client, understanding their lifestyle, preferences, and expectations. we discovered that they loved traveling and had a fondness for luxury hotels. this insight helped us design a bedroom that reflected their love for luxury and comfort. we also created a

  • mood board
  • color palette
  • furniture layout
to visualize the design concept and ensure that everyone was on the same page.

the design solution

after careful consideration and planning, we presented the client with a design solution that met their requirements. we opted for a

"less is more" approach, focusing on clean lines, minimal decor, and an emphasis on texture and materiality. this approach not only helped us stay within the budget but also created a sense of calm and serenity in the room.
we selected a combination of rich fabrics, plush carpets, and elegant furniture pieces that exuded luxury and sophistication. the total cost of the project was ₹20 lakh, which was within the client's budget.

the result

the final result was a stunning bedroom that exceeded the client's expectations. the room was filled with natural light, and the carefully selected furniture and decor created a sense of warmth and comfort. the client was thrilled with the outcome and appreciated the attention to detail that our team at indéva studio had brought to the project. if you're looking for a similar design solution, you can explore our services or get in touch with our designers to discuss your project.
