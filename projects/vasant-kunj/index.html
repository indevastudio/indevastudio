Residential · New Delhi · 2025

Vasant Kunj

A parents' bedroom built on walnut fluting, warm beige lacquer, and brushed brass — quiet sophistication resolved through joinery, not decoration.

LocationNew Delhi
AreaDDA Residence
Duration2 months
ScopeInterior Design + Custom Joinery + Execution
StatusCompleted

the brief

Achieving a sophisticated, quiet material palette resolved almost entirely through joinery and lighting — with no reliance on applied decoration.

the approach

Every surface — the fluted headboard wall, the backlit TV panel, the window seat — was drawn before it was built. Lighting was layered, not applied: cove, panel and pendant sources let the room shift from morning clarity to evening intimacy.
