Vendor Standards & Material Handbook

Our Vendors

The manufacturers Indéva Studio specifies for luxury residential, farmhouse, commercial and hospitality interiors across India — chosen for craftsmanship, after-sales support, and long-term reliability, not for price.
2026 Edition

Indéva Studio designs from structure before decoration — and that discipline extends to the vendors we let onto a project. A material is never chosen because it photographs well; it is chosen because it will still perform after a decade of daily use. This handbook is our internal reference for the brands we trust enough to specify on behalf of our clients.

INDÉVA STUDIO

We do not procure on price. We procure on total cost of ownership — the sum of the material cost, the service cost, and the cost of a client's trust if something fails prematurely. Where a premium brand's after-sales support materially reduces long-term risk, that brand is our default specification, even where a cheaper alternative exists.
