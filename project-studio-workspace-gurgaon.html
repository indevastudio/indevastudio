Studio Workspace, Gurgaon

A 3,200 sq ft workplace in Gurgaon designed for sustained focus: workstations, meeting rooms and a cafeteria on a mid-size floor plate that never feels corridor-driven.

The brief

Fit focused individual workstations, meeting rooms and a cafeteria into a mid-size floor plate without the office feeling cramped.

Design approach

Daylight drove the layout. Workstations were placed first, along the light; everything else followed. Conference rooms were sized for their actual use, not for appearance.

Spaces in this project

Office entrance, main studio floor, workstation zone, conference room and cafeteria.
