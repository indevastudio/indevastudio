contemporary interior design trends india 2025: a weekend retreat in sonipat

in a bustling city like delhi, it's common for people to seek a peaceful retreat from the chaos. for one such client, indéva studio was approached to design a weekend retreat in kmp expressway, sonipat, with a budget of ₹8-15 lakh. the client wanted a contemporary and sustainable design that would serve as a perfect getaway from the city. the project timeline was set for 12 weeks, from january 15, 2025, to april 7, 2025.

the brief

the client's brief was to design a 2bhk weekend retreat with a total area of 1200 sqft. the client wanted the design to be contemporary, sustainable, and functional, with a focus on natural light and ventilation. the client also wanted to incorporate elements of indian architecture and design, while keeping the overall aesthetic modern and sleek.

initial discussions

during the initial discussions, the client mentioned their love for nature and the outdoors. they wanted the design to reflect this, with plenty of natural elements and materials used throughout the retreat. the client also expressed their concern for the environment and wanted the design to be sustainable and eco-friendly.

the challenge

one of the major challenges faced by the indéva studio team was to design a contemporary and sustainable retreat within the given budget of ₹8-15 lakh. the team had to carefully select materials and design elements that would not only meet the client's requirements but also fit within the budget. another challenge was to ensure that the design was functional and met the client's needs, while also being aesthetically pleasing.

site analysis

the indéva studio team conducted a site analysis to understand the natural surroundings and the climate of the area. the team noticed that the site received plenty of natural light throughout the day, which could be utilized to reduce the need for artificial lighting. the team also observed that the site was surrounded by plenty of greenery, which could be incorporated into the design to create a natural and peaceful ambiance.

the design solution

based on the client's brief and the site analysis, the indéva studio team came up with a design solution that met all the requirements. the design featured large windows and doors that allowed plenty of natural light and ventilation into the retreat. the team used sustainable materials such as reclaimed wood, bamboo, and low-voc paints to reduce the environmental impact of the design. the team also incorporated elements of indian architecture and design, such as the use of jaali screens and intricate carvings.

  • large windows and doors for natural light and ventilation
  • sustainable materials such as reclaimed wood, bamboo, and low-voc paints
  • elements of indian architecture and design, such as jaali screens and intricate carvings
the key to a successful design is to understand the client's needs and preferences, and to create a design that not only meets but exceeds their expectations. in this case, the client wanted a contemporary and sustainable design that would serve as a perfect getaway from the city, and we were able to deliver that within the given budget of ₹12 lakh.

the result

the final result was a stunning weekend retreat that met all the client's requirements. the design was contemporary, sustainable, and functional, with plenty of natural elements and materials used throughout. the client was thrilled with the final result and praised the indéva studio team for their attention to detail and commitment to sustainability. the total cost of the project came out to be ₹12 lakh, which was within the given budget.

client feedback

the client was extremely satisfied with the final result and praised the indéva studio team for their professionalism and expertise. the client mentioned that the design exceeded their expectations and that they were thrilled with the final result. the client also appreciated the team's attention to detail and commitment to sustainability.

frequently asked questions

what is the importance of sustainable design in interior design?

sustainable design is important in interior design as it reduces the environmental impact of the design and creates a healthier and more sustainable space for the occupants. sustainable design elements such as natural materials, energy-efficient lighting, and low-voc paints can improve indoor air quality and reduce the carbon footprint of the building.

how can i incorporate elements of indian architecture and design into my interior design?

you can incorporate elements of indian architecture and design into your interior design by using traditional indian materials and design elements such as jaali screens, intricate carvings, and colorful textiles. you can also use indian-inspired patterns and motifs in your furniture and decor to add a touch of indian design to your space.

what is the role of natural light and ventilation in interior design?

natural light and ventilation play a crucial role in interior design as they can improve the mood and productivity of the occupants. natural light can also reduce the need for artificial lighting, which can save energy and reduce the carbon footprint of the building. proper ventilation can also improve indoor air quality and reduce the risk of health problems.
