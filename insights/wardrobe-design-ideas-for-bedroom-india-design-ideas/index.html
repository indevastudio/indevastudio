wardrobe design ideas for bedroom india: a journey of elegance

Imagine stepping into a serene bedroom in a luxury apartment in DLF Phase 5, Gurgaon, where the warm glow of the setting sun casts a golden light on the rich, dark wood tones of a beautifully crafted wardrobe. The room is a masterful blend of traditional Indian aesthetics and modern luxury, with intricate carvings and ornate details that evoke the opulence of the Mughal era. As you approach the wardrobe, you notice the subtle play of light on the polished surfaces, the soft sheen of the hardware, and the delicate patterns etched into the glass panels. This is the epitome of luxury wardrobe design in India, where every element comes together to create a space that is both breathtakingly beautiful and profoundly functional.

traditional indian wardrobe design

This style is perfect for those who want to incorporate traditional Indian elements into their bedroom design. Think intricate carvings, ornate details, and rich, dark wood tones. The cost range for this style can vary from ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000, depending on the materials and craftsmanship. For example, a beautifully crafted wooden wardrobe with intricate carvings can cost around ₹1,50,000, while a simpler design with less intricate details can cost around ₹80,000.

Who it suits: This style is ideal for those who appreciate traditional Indian aesthetics and want to create a warm, inviting atmosphere in their bedroom.

modern minimalist wardrobe design

For a more modern and sleek look, consider a minimalist wardrobe design. This style features clean lines, simple shapes, and a limited color palette. The cost range for this style can vary from ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000, depending on the materials and design. For example, a simple, wall-mounted wardrobe with a minimalist design can cost around ₹50,000, while a more complex design with sliding doors and internal shelves can cost around ₹1,20,000.

Who it suits: This style is perfect for those who prefer a clean, uncluttered look and want to create a sense of calm in their bedroom.

materials and textures

When it comes to materials and textures, the options are endless. From rich, dark wood tones to sleek, high-gloss finishes, the choice of material can greatly impact the overall look and feel of the wardrobe. Some popular materials for wardrobe design include:

  • Wood: ₹20,000 - ₹50,000 per unit
  • MDF: ₹10,000 - ₹30,000 per unit
  • Plywood: ₹15,000 - ₹40,000 per unit
  • Glass: ₹30,000 - ₹80,000 per unit
According to the national institute of design, the choice of material can greatly impact the overall sustainability of the design. When choosing materials, consider the environmental impact and opt for sustainable options whenever possible.

luxury wardrobe design with sliding doors

For a truly luxurious look, consider a wardrobe design with sliding doors. This style features large, floor-to-ceiling doors that slide effortlessly on tracks, creating a sense of drama and sophistication. The cost range for this style can vary from ₹80,000 to ₹3,00,000, depending on the materials and design. For example, a simple design with two sliding doors can cost around ₹1,20,000, while a more complex design with three or four doors can cost around ₹2,50,000.

Who it suits: This style is perfect for those who want to create a sense of luxury and drama in their bedroom.
