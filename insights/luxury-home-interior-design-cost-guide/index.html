cost guide to luxury home interior design

august 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

the cost of luxury: navigating the world of high-end home interior design

when it comes to luxury home interior design, one of the biggest misconceptions in india is that it's only for the ultra-rich. however, with the right budget and planning, anyone can achieve their dream home. in fact, a well-designed home can even increase its value over time. for instance, an independent bungalow in maharani bagh, delhi, with a budget of ₹1–3 crore, can be a worthwhile investment.

what drives costs up in luxury home interior design?

several factors contribute to the high costs of luxury home interior design, including the use of high-end materials, custom furniture, and intricate design elements. for example, a single piece of bespoke furniture can cost anywhere from ₹50,000 to ₹500,000 or more, depending on the design, materials, and craftsmanship. additionally, the cost of hiring a professional home interior designer, such as a home interior designer in maharani bagh, can range from ₹5 lakh to ₹20 lakh or more, depending on their experience and expertise.

cost breakdown by room

to better understand the costs involved, let's break down the estimated expenses for each room in a luxury home. for a budget of ₹1–3 crore, here's a rough estimate of what you might expect to pay for each room:

  • living room: ₹10 lakh to ₹30 lakh
  • kitchen: ₹15 lakh to ₹50 lakh
  • bedroom: ₹8 lakh to ₹25 lakh
  • bathroom: ₹5 lakh to ₹15 lakh
keep in mind that these are rough estimates and can vary greatly depending on the specific design elements, materials, and furniture chosen.

the importance of budgeting

creating a budget is essential to ensuring that your luxury home interior design project stays on track. it's essential to consider all the costs involved, including materials, labor, and design fees. a well-planned budget can help you avoid costly surprises and ensure that your dream home is completed within your means.

the key to successful luxury home interior design is not just about spending money, but about investing in quality and craftsmanship that will stand the test of time.

how to negotiate with your home interior designer

when working with a home interior designer, it's essential to communicate your budget and expectations clearly. be sure to ask about their design process, fees, and any additional costs that may be involved. it's also important to negotiate and agree on a budget that works for both you and your designer. for instance, you can ask about any package deals or discounts they may offer, such as a discount for a full-home design project.

red flags that inflate bills

there are several red flags to watch out for when working with a home interior designer that can inflate your bills. these include:

  • hidden fees or charges
  • low-quality materials or craftsmanship
  • poor communication or project management
be sure to research your designer thoroughly and read reviews from previous clients to ensure you're working with a reputable and trustworthy professional.
