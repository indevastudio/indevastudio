what drives costs up

design complexity

Every curve, custom millwork, or unique lighting fixture adds engineering hours. A simple linear layout may cost 10‑15% of the total budget, while a bespoke design can climb to 25% or more.

material selection

Premium marble, imported wood, and smart home systems are the biggest budget busters. For example, a 30 sq ft marble slab from Italy can cost ₹12,000 per sq ft, whereas locally sourced quartz runs around ₹4,000 per sq ft.

site conditions

Older buildings often need structural reinforcement, waterproofing, or hidden MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) upgrades. These hidden layers can add ₹5‑10 lakh to the final bill.

cost breakdown by room

  • living room: ₹8 – 12 lakh – includes statement furniture, flooring, and ambient lighting.
  • master bedroom: ₹6 – 9 lakh – covers custom wardrobes, headboard, and soft furnishings.
  • kitchen & dining: ₹7 – 11 lakh – cabinetry, premium countertops, and integrated appliances.
  • service areas (bathroom, pantry): ₹4 – 6 lakh – waterproofing, fixtures, and finishes.

These figures assume a mid‑range selection of materials and a design narrative that reflects luxury without excessive extravagance.

how to negotiate without compromising style

Negotiation is not about cutting corners; it’s about aligning expectations with realistic cost structures.

“asking for a detailed cost matrix before signing any contract is the single most effective way to avoid surprise invoices.” – industry analyst

Follow these steps:

  1. Request a line‑item quotation that separates design, material, and labor.
  2. Identify high‑impact items (e.g., custom furniture) and explore alternative finishes that offer similar aesthetics at lower cost.
  3. Leverage bulk purchasing power. indéva studio often groups orders for multiple projects, passing volume discounts to clients.
  4. Set a clear cap for contingencies—typically 5‑10% of the total budget.

red flags that inflate bills

Even reputable firms can encounter scope creep if homeowners are not vigilant.

  • Unexplained “admin fees” that appear after the contract is signed.
  • Frequent revisions to the design plan without documented change orders.
  • Materials listed as “premium” without brand or specification details.
  • Labor charges that increase hourly without a pre‑agreed schedule.

When any of these appear, pause the project and ask for a revised estimate. Transparent firms will provide a written justification.
