weekend farmhouse interior design mistakes to avoid in gurgaon

the day a client called us mid-project, frantic about the ₹50 lakh overspend on their weekend farmhouse in sector 150, noida, was the day we realized the importance of careful planning in luxury interior design. as a senior writer for indéva studio, i have seen many clients fall into common traps, resulting in costly mistakes and disappointed expectations.

mistake 1: inadequate space planning

what goes wrong: a poorly planned layout can lead to wasted space, inefficient use of resources, and a decrease in the overall value of the property. why it happens: often, clients prioritize aesthetics over functionality, neglecting the importance of a well-designed floor plan. exact fix: work with an experienced designer to create a customized layout that meets your specific needs and budget.

importance of measurement

  • accurate measurements are crucial to ensure a smooth installation process
  • a well-measured space can help identify potential issues before they become major problems
  • investing in a thorough measurement process can save you up to ₹10 lakh in the long run

mistake 2: ignoring sustainability

what goes wrong: neglecting sustainable design principles can result in increased energy consumption, higher maintenance costs, and a negative impact on the environment. why it happens: clients often underestimate the importance of sustainability in luxury interior design. exact fix: incorporate eco-friendly materials, energy-efficient systems, and green building practices into your design, such as those recommended by the indian green building council.

as a designer, it's our responsibility to educate clients about the benefits of sustainable design and help them make informed decisions that not only save them money but also contribute to a healthier planet.

mistake 3: overspending on unnecessary features

what goes wrong: overspending on luxury features can quickly blow your budget, leaving you with a partially completed project. why it happens: clients often get caught up in the excitement of designing their dream home and lose sight of their budget constraints. exact fix: prioritize your needs, and allocate your ₹1-3 crore budget wisely, focusing on essential elements that will enhance the overall functionality and aesthetic of your weekend farmhouse.

mistake 4: poor material selection

what goes wrong: choosing low-quality materials can lead to premature wear and tear, requiring costly repairs and replacements. why it happens: clients may opt for cheaper materials to save money upfront, unaware of the long-term consequences. exact fix: invest in high-quality, durable materials that meet your design and functional requirements, even if it means spending a bit more initially.

mistake 5: inadequate lighting design

what goes wrong: insufficient or poorly designed lighting can create an uninviting atmosphere, making your weekend farmhouse feel unwelcoming. why it happens: clients often overlook the importance of lighting in their design plans. exact fix: work with a professional designer to create a comprehensive lighting plan that balances ambiance, functionality, and energy efficiency.
