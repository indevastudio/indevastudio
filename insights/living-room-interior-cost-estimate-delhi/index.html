living room interior cost estimate delhi: mistakes to avoid

not accounting for hidden costs

What goes wrong: Hidden costs can quickly add up, blowing your budget and causing unnecessary stress. Why it happens: Homeowners often overlook expenses like electrical and plumbing work, flooring, and painting, which can be costly. Exact fix: Work with an experienced designer who can help you identify potential hidden costs and factor them into your budget from the start.

insufficient research and planning

What goes wrong: Without proper research and planning, your project can stall, and costs can escalate. Why it happens: Homeowners often dive into a project without researching the market, understanding the scope of work, or creating a realistic timeline. Exact fix: Take the time to research, plan, and create a detailed scope of work, including a realistic timeline and budget. Consider consulting with a professional designer or visiting websites like national institute of design to get a better understanding of the process.

creating a realistic timeline

  • Break down the project into smaller, manageable tasks
  • Establish a realistic timeline for each task
  • Identify potential roadblocks and have a contingency plan in place
As a designer, it's essential to understand that a well-planned project is not just about aesthetics; it's also about creating a functional and comfortable space that meets the client's needs and budget.

not prioritizing quality over cost

What goes wrong: Compromising on quality to save costs can result in a subpar finish and reduced longevity of the materials. Why it happens: Homeowners often prioritize cost over quality, which can lead to costly repairs and replacements in the long run. Exact fix: Invest in high-quality materials and work with experienced professionals who can help you achieve the desired look and feel while staying within your budget of ₹1-3 crore.

not considering the location and climate

What goes wrong: Not considering the location and climate can result in a space that's not functional or comfortable. Why it happens: Homeowners often overlook the impact of the location and climate on their design choices. Exact fix: Work with a designer who understands the local climate and can help you create a space that's tailored to your needs and the unique conditions of Sector 150, Noida.
