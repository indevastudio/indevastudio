restaurant ambience design cost india: a case study

in a bustling city like south delhi, delhi, creating a unique restaurant ambience can be a challenge, especially when working with a budget of ₹15-25 lakh. our client, a restaurateur with a passion for indian cuisine, approached indéva studio to design a restaurant that would stand out from the crowd.

the brief

our client had an independent bungalow in south delhi, which they wanted to convert into a restaurant. the brief was to create a unique ambience that would reflect the warmth and hospitality of indian culture, while also ensuring that the space was functional and efficient. the client had a budget of ₹15-25 lakh and wanted the project to be completed within 12 weeks.

the challenge

the biggest challenge we faced was creating a unique ambience within the given budget. we had to carefully select materials and design elements that would not only reflect the indian culture but also be cost-effective. another challenge was ensuring that the space was functional and efficient, while also creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.

design decisions

to address these challenges, we made several design decisions. we decided to use a combination of natural materials such as wood and stone, along with vibrant colors and textures to create a warm and inviting atmosphere. we also used energy-efficient lighting and equipment to reduce the carbon footprint of the restaurant and ensure that it was certified by the indian green building council.

the design solution

our design solution was to create a restaurant that was divided into different zones, each with its own unique character and ambience. we used a combination of seating areas, including booths, tables, and a bar, to create a flexible and functional space. we also incorporated indian elements such as intricate carvings, colorful textiles, and traditional artwork to reflect the warmth and hospitality of indian culture.

  • used natural materials such as wood and stone to create a warm and inviting atmosphere
  • incorporated vibrant colors and textures to add depth and visual interest
  • used energy-efficient lighting and equipment to reduce the carbon footprint of the restaurant
the key to creating a unique restaurant ambience is to understand the client's vision and preferences, and to use design elements that reflect their brand and culture. in this case, our client wanted to create a warm and inviting atmosphere that reflected the hospitality of indian culture, and we were able to achieve this through our design solution.

the result

the final result was a restaurant that was not only unique and inviting but also functional and efficient. the client was thrilled with the outcome, and the restaurant quickly became a popular destination in south delhi. the project was completed within the given timeline of 12 weeks, and the final cost was ₹20 lakh, which was within the client's budget of ₹15-25 lakh.
