the surprising truth about villa interior designers in delhi ncr

did you know that the biggest cost misconception in india is that interior design services are a luxury only the wealthy can afford? in reality, a well-planned interior design can save you up to ₹5 lakh in the long run by optimizing space and reducing waste. for a plotted home in rai, sonipat, with a budget of ₹25–50 lakh, it's essential to understand the costs involved in hiring a villa interior designer in delhi ncr.

what drives up the costs of villa interior design?

several factors contribute to the overall cost of villa interior design, including the size of the property, materials used, and the designer's fee. on average, a villa interior designer in delhi ncr can charge between ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh, depending on their experience and the scope of work. for a budget of ₹25–50 lakh, you can expect to pay around ₹1.5 lakh to ₹3 lakh for design services.

cost breakdown by room

to give you a better idea of the costs involved, here's a breakdown of the estimated expenses for each room in your villa:

  • living room: ₹2 lakh to ₹5 lakh
  • kitchen: ₹1.5 lakh to ₹3 lakh
  • bedrooms: ₹1 lakh to ₹2 lakh each
  • bathrooms: ₹50,000 to ₹1 lakh each

additional costs to consider

in addition to the design services and materials, you should also consider the costs of furniture, fixtures, and appliances. these can range from ₹50,000 to ₹2 lakh, depending on your choices.
