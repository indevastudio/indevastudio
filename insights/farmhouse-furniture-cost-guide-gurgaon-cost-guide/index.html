how much does farmhouse furniture design in gurgaon really cost?

september 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

A recent industry survey showed that 68% of homeowners overestimate the cost of a high‑end farmhouse look, assuming the price tag must exceed ₹1 crore.

what drives costs up

Understanding the real cost drivers helps avoid surprise invoices. In Gurgaon, the following factors have the biggest impact:

  • Material selection: Solid teak, reclaimed oak, and custom‑finished metal can double the price of a standard pine piece.
  • Customization level: Intricate joinery, built‑in lighting, and modular configurations add design hours and tooling expenses.
  • Logistics and handling: Heavy furniture for a Gurgaon farmhouse often requires special transport, especially when navigating narrow lanes in upscale colonies.
  • Finishing & detailing: Hand‑stained or hand‑painted finishes demand skilled artisans, raising labour costs.
  • Brand premium: Working with a top‑tier interior firm like indéva studio adds a design fee that reflects expertise, not just material markup.

cost breakdown by room

Breaking the budget down room by room gives a realistic picture. The figures below are based on a typical luxury apartment in Gurgaon, with an overall budget range of ₹40–80 lakh.

living area

Key pieces include a statement sofa, coffee table, and media console. Expect:

  • Custom sofa set: ₹6–10 lakh
  • Hand‑crafted coffee table: ₹2–4 lakh
  • Media console with concealed wiring: ₹3–5 lakh

dining space

A farmhouse vibe often uses a large wooden table with matching chairs.

  • Extendable oak dining table: ₹5–9 lakh
  • Set of 8 upholstered chairs: ₹2–3 lakh

bedroom suite

Focus on a statement bed frame, bedside tables, and a wardrobe.

  • Carved teak headboard: ₹4–7 lakh
  • Custom wardrobe with sliding doors: ₹5–8 lakh
  • Nightstands (pair): ₹1.5–2.5 lakh

outdoor terrace / balcony

Even a private terrace can carry the farmhouse aesthetic.

  • Weather‑proof lounge set: ₹2–4 lakh
  • Planter boxes with integrated lighting: ₹0.5–1 lakh

Adding up these core pieces places the total furniture spend between ₹30 and ₹55 lakh, leaving ₹10–25 lakh for accessories, soft furnishings, and the designer’s service fee.

how to negotiate wisely

Negotiation is not about cutting corners; it’s about aligning expectations with realistic costs.

“A transparent cost sheet builds trust faster than a lower quote that later balloons with hidden fees.” – senior procurement analyst, Gurgaon.

Follow these steps:

  1. Request a detailed cost sheet: Break down each item, labour, transport, and taxes.
  2. Set a fixed‑fee ceiling: Agree on a maximum amount for design services before work begins.
  3. Leverage bulk purchasing: Ordering multiple pieces from the same supplier often unlocks volume discounts.
  4. Ask for phased payments: Tie payments to milestones such as design approval, procurement, and installation.
  5. Consider alternative finishes: Switching from hand‑stained to factory‑finished can shave 10–15% off the price.

red flags that inflate bills

Even reputable firms can encounter cost creep if warning signs are ignored.

  • Vague quotations that list “materials as required” without specifics.
  • Repeated changes in design scope without documented change orders.
  • Unexplained “site‑visit fees” after the initial survey.
  • Use of subcontractors without clear accountability.
  • Absence of a signed agreement detailing warranty and after‑service terms.

Spotting these early prevents the budget from spiralling beyond the ₹80 lakh ceiling.

frequently asked questions

Q1: How much of the budget should be reserved for design fees?

Typically, 10–15% of the total project cost covers the designer’s fee, including concept development, sourcing, and project supervision.

Q2: Can I use locally sourced wood to lower costs?

Yes. Choosing locally sourced, sustainably harvested wood can reduce material costs by 8–12% while still delivering a farmhouse aesthetic.

Q3: Is it worth paying extra for hand‑crafted finishes?

Hand‑crafted finishes add character and durability, but the premium can be justified only if the client values uniqueness over cost savings.

Q4: How does interior design in gurgaon differ from other markets?

Gurgaon clients often demand a blend of modern luxury and traditional warmth, leading designers to balance high‑end finishes with farmhouse charm.
