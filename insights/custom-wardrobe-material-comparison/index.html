understanding custom wardrobe costs in gurgaon: a material guide

september 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

The most common misconception is that all wardrobes cost the same once the design is set; in reality, the material choice drives the majority of the price variance.

material properties: plywood vs. MDF vs. solid wood

When a client in Delhi NCR, Delhi NCR requests a bespoke storage solution, indéva studio typically evaluates three core material families:

  • Plywood: layers of thin wood veneers glued together, offering balanced strength and weight.
  • MDF (Medium‑Density Fiberboard): compressed wood fibers bound with resin, known for a smooth surface that accepts paint well.
  • Solid wood: natural timber cut from a single piece, providing unique grain patterns and the highest structural integrity.

Each option has distinct characteristics that affect load‑bearing capacity, moisture resistance, and finish options. Plywood’s cross‑grain construction makes it less prone to warping, while MDF can swell if exposed to humidity. Solid wood, though resistant to moisture, can be vulnerable to termite activity if not properly treated.

where each option makes sense

Understanding the use‑case helps narrow the decision:

  • Budget‑sensitive projects: MDF offers the lowest upfront cost but demands strict moisture control. Ideal for interiors where the wardrobe is away from kitchens or bathrooms.
  • Mid‑range luxury: Plywood balances cost and durability, making it the default for most our services in Delhi NCR interior design. It accepts a variety of veneers and laminates, allowing designers to achieve high‑end looks without the price of solid timber.
  • Heritage or premium homes: Solid wood is chosen for its timeless appeal and structural strength. It justifies the higher expense in properties where the wardrobe is a focal point of the bedroom suite.

what indéva studio specifies by default and why

For most residential commissions, indéva studio recommends a 15 mm marine plywood core with a high‑gloss veneer. The rationale includes:

“A marine-grade plywood core provides consistent strength while resisting the occasional humidity spikes that are common in Delhi NCR homes, delivering a lifespan that matches the overall project budget.”

This specification aligns with the studio’s commitment to delivering a reliable, low‑maintenance wardrobe that still feels luxurious. The veneer can be sourced from sustainable forests, meeting the growing demand for eco‑friendly materials without compromising on aesthetics.

frequently asked questions

Q1? How does the choice of material affect the overall interior design budget?

The material accounts for 40‑60 % of the wardrobe cost. Selecting a higher‑priced material like solid wood can increase the wardrobe portion of a ₹2 crore interior budget by up to 3 %, which may require adjustments elsewhere.

Q2? Is MDF ever a suitable choice for humid climates?

Only if the wardrobe is sealed with a waterproof laminate and installed away from moisture sources. Even then, regular maintenance checks are advisable.

Q3? What finishes are compatible with plywood?

Plywood works well with veneers, laminates, and high‑pressure laminates (HPL). It can also be painted with water‑based paints after proper priming.

Q4? How can termite risk be mitigated for solid wood wardrobes?

Applying a borate‑based preservative during fabrication and ensuring a gap of at least 5 cm from the floor can significantly reduce risk.
