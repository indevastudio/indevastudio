couple bedroom interior design delhi: a step-by-step guide

most people approach couple bedroom interior design backwards. here is the right sequence. when it comes to designing a couple bedroom in delhi, especially in a luxurious area like greater kailash, delhi, the first question that comes to mind is: how do i create a space that reflects my personal style and meets my needs within a budget of ₹40-80 lakh?

step 1: define your budget and requirements

to start, it's essential to define your budget and requirements. what are your must-haves in a couple bedroom? do you want a spacious closet, a comfortable seating area, or a luxurious bathroom? make a list of your priorities and allocate your budget accordingly. you can expect to spend around ₹50-70 lakh for a luxurious couple bedroom in delhi.

what to do: create a list of your requirements and prioritize them. what to ask your designer: how can we allocate the budget to meet my requirements? common pitfall: not prioritizing your requirements can lead to overspending on non-essential items.

step 2: plan the layout and design

once you have defined your budget and requirements, it's time to plan the layout and design of your couple bedroom. consider the size and shape of the room, as well as the natural light and ventilation. you can find inspiration from various sources, including elle decor india and our portfolio.

understanding the design process

at indéva studio, we follow a rigorous design process to ensure that our clients' needs are met. our team of expert designers will work with you to create a customized design plan that reflects your personal style and meets your requirements.

what to do: gather inspiration and ideas for your design. what to ask your designer: how can we create a functional and beautiful layout? common pitfall: not considering the natural light and ventilation can lead to a dull and uncomfortable space.

step 3: select materials and finishes

with the design plan in place, it's time to select the materials and finishes for your couple bedroom. this includes everything from the type of flooring to the color of the walls. consider factors such as durability, maintenance, and aesthetics when making your selections.

  • flooring options: hardwood, marble, or carpet
  • wall finishes: paint, wallpaper, or paneling
  • furniture: custom-made or ready-to-use

what to do: research and select materials and finishes that meet your requirements. what to ask your designer: how can we ensure that the materials and finishes are durable and easy to maintain? common pitfall: not considering the maintenance requirements can lead to costly repairs down the line.

step 4: execute the design plan

once the design plan and materials have been finalized, it's time to execute the plan. this involves working with contractors and suppliers to bring the design to life. at indéva studio, we work closely with our clients to ensure that the execution process is smooth and hassle-free.

the key to a successful execution is to have a clear plan and a experienced team to carry it out. at indéva studio, we have a team of experts who will work with you to ensure that your couple bedroom is completed on time and within budget.

what to do: work closely with your designer and contractors to ensure a smooth execution. what to ask your designer: how can we ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget? common pitfall: not having a clear plan can lead to delays and cost overruns.
