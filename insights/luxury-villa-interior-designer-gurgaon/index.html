luxury villa interior design: a case study on golf course road, gurgaon

our client, a young family, had recently purchased a villa on golf course road, gurgaon, and were looking to give it a complete makeover. with a budget of ₹25–50 lakh, they wanted a unique and functional space that reflected their personality and style. after researching various interior design firms, they decided to get in touch with our team at indéva studio.

the brief

the client's brief was to create a luxurious and modern interior design that would cater to their lifestyle. they wanted a spacious living area, a formal dining room, and four bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom. they also desired a home theater, a gym, and a private study. our team at indéva studio was excited to take on this challenge and create a bespoke design that met the client's needs and exceeded their expectations.

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges we faced was the tight budget of ₹25–50 lakh. we had to be creative and resourceful to achieve the client's vision without compromising on quality. another challenge was the villa's existing layout, which required significant modifications to meet the client's requirements. our team worked closely with the client to understand their priorities and developed a phased design approach to ensure that the project stayed within budget.

space planning and layout

we began by analyzing the villa's floor plan and identifying areas that could be optimized for better functionality. we decided to merge two smaller rooms to create a large home theater, and converted a spare room into a private study. we also redesigned the kitchen to include a breakfast nook and a separate dining area.

  • merged two smaller rooms to create a large home theater
  • converted a spare room into a private study
  • redesigned the kitchen to include a breakfast nook and a separate dining area

the design solution

our design solution was to create a luxurious and modern interior that incorporated the client's personal style and preferences. we selected a neutral color palette with accents of warm beige and rich wood tones to create a cozy and inviting atmosphere. we also incorporated natural materials, such as marble and stone, to add texture and depth to the space.

as noted by architectural digest india, "the use of natural materials can add a sense of warmth and character to a space, making it feel more inviting and personalized."

the result

the final result was a stunning luxury villa that exceeded the client's expectations. the interior design was modern, luxurious, and functional, with each room carefully designed to meet the client's specific needs. the client was thrilled with the outcome and appreciated the attention to detail and the creative solutions we provided to stay within budget. the total cost of the project came in at ₹35 lakh, well within the client's budget of ₹25–50 lakh.
