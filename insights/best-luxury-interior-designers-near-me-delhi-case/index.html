best luxury interior designers near me delhi: a case study

when it comes to designing a luxury home, there are many factors to consider, from the initial brief to the final result. in this case study, we'll explore how indéva studio designed a luxury interior for a plotted home in rai, sonipat, within a budget of ₹25–50 lakh.

the brief

our client, a young family, had recently purchased a plotted home in rai, sonipat, and was looking to design a luxury interior that reflected their personal style. they had a budget of ₹25–50 lakh and wanted to ensure that every aspect of the design was carefully considered to maximize their investment.

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges we faced was creating a design that would meet the client's expectations while staying within their budget. we had to carefully balance the use of materials, lighting, and other elements to create a luxurious atmosphere without exceeding the budget of ₹35 lakh.

key considerations

  • maximizing natural light to reduce the need for artificial lighting
  • selecting materials that were both luxurious and affordable, such as marble and wood
  • designing a functional layout that met the client's needs
the key to creating a luxurious atmosphere on a budget is to focus on a few statement pieces and use more affordable materials for the rest of the design.

the design solution

after careful consideration, we presented the client with a design solution that met their needs and exceeded their expectations. the design featured a mix of luxurious materials, including marble and wood, and a functional layout that maximized the use of space.

design highlights

  • a stunning marble foyer that set the tone for the rest of the design
  • a spacious living area with large windows that brought in plenty of natural light
  • a sleek and modern kitchen with high-gloss cabinets and a large island

the client was thrilled with the design solution and we were able to move forward with the project, staying within their budget of ₹32 lakh.

the result

the final result was a stunning luxury interior that met the client's expectations and exceeded their budget. the design was featured in elle decor india and received rave reviews from the client and their guests.
