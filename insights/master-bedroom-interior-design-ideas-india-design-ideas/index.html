master bedroom interior design ideas india

imagine stepping into a serene and luxurious master bedroom, surrounded by the tranquility of nature, with large windows overlooking the lush greenery of Golf Course Road, Gurgaon. the room is filled with warm, golden light, and the air is crisp and clean, inviting you to relax and unwind after a long day. the bed, a plush and comfortable haven, is dressed in crisp, white linens, and adorned with intricate, handmade embroidery, reflecting the rich cultural heritage of india.

traditional indian design

a traditional indian design is perfect for those who appreciate the rich cultural heritage of our country. this style features intricate carvings, ornate patterns, and vibrant colors, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. materials such as teak wood, marble, and silk are commonly used, and the cost range for this design can vary from ₹30-45 lakh. this style suits those who value tradition and want to incorporate indian aesthetics into their master bedroom design.

modern minimalism

for those who prefer a more modern and minimalist approach, a clean and simple design can be achieved with the use of materials such as glass, steel, and concrete. this style features a neutral color palette, minimal ornamentation, and an emphasis on functionality. the cost range for this design can vary from ₹25-40 lakh. this style suits those who value simplicity and want a low-maintenance, easy-to-clean space.

key elements

  • neutral color palette
  • minimal ornamentation
  • emphasis on functionality
as the national institute of design suggests, a well-designed space should be a reflection of the user's personality and lifestyle. at indéva studio, we believe in creating bespoke designs that cater to the unique needs and preferences of our clients.

luxurious elegance

a luxurious and elegant design is perfect for those who want to create a sophisticated and opulent atmosphere in their master bedroom. this style features rich, bold colors, intricate patterns, and ornate details, creating a sense of grandeur and luxury. materials such as velvet, silk, and marble are commonly used, and the cost range for this design can vary from ₹40-50 lakh. this style suits those who value luxury and want to create a statement piece in their home.

eco-friendly design

for those who are environmentally conscious, an eco-friendly design can be achieved with the use of sustainable materials, such as reclaimed wood, bamboo, and low-voc paints. this style features a natural color palette, minimal waste, and an emphasis on energy efficiency. the cost range for this design can vary from ₹25-40 lakh. this style suits those who value sustainability and want to reduce their carbon footprint.
