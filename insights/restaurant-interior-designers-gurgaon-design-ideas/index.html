restaurant interior designers gurgaon: curated design ideas

Imagine walking into a cozy restaurant in an independent bungalow in South Delhi, Delhi, with a warm and inviting atmosphere that immediately makes you feel at home. The walls are adorned with traditional Indian patterns, and the furniture is a perfect blend of modern and vintage pieces. The aroma of freshly cooked food wafts through the air, making your stomach growl with anticipation. This is what a well-designed restaurant can do - create an immersive experience that leaves a lasting impression on its customers.

modern minimalist design

A modern minimalist design is perfect for those who want a clean and sleek look for their restaurant. This style features a neutral color palette, simple furniture, and minimal decor. The use of materials like glass, steel, and concrete adds to the modern feel. The cost of implementing this design can range from ₹15 lakh to ₹20 lakh, depending on the size of the restaurant and the materials used. This style suits restaurants that want to appeal to a young and urban crowd.

traditional indian design

For those who want to incorporate traditional Indian elements into their restaurant design, this style is a great option. This design features intricate patterns, vibrant colors, and ornate furniture. The use of materials like wood, stone, and fabrics adds to the traditional feel. The cost of implementing this design can range from ₹18 lakh to ₹22 lakh, depending on the complexity of the design and the materials used. This style suits restaurants that want to showcase Indian culture and heritage.

incorporating indian aesthetics

Indian aesthetics can be incorporated into the design through the use of traditional patterns, colors, and motifs. For example, the use of block printing, embroidery, or tie-dye can add a unique touch to the furniture and decor. The incorporation of Indian elements can also be done through the use of traditional Indian materials like jali, jaali, or carved wood.

eco-friendly design

An eco-friendly design is perfect for those who want to create a sustainable and environmentally friendly restaurant. This style features the use of natural materials, energy-efficient lighting, and eco-friendly decor. The cost of implementing this design can range from ₹20 lakh to ₹25 lakh, depending on the complexity of the design and the materials used. This style suits restaurants that want to appeal to the environmentally conscious crowd. For more information on eco-friendly design, you can visit the indian green building council website.

  • use of natural materials like bamboo, reclaimed wood, and low-voc paints
  • energy-efficient lighting like led lights and solar panels
  • eco-friendly decor like plants, recycled materials, and sustainable fabrics
the key to creating a successful eco-friendly design is to strike a balance between sustainability and aesthetics. it's not just about using natural materials, but also about creating a beautiful and inviting space that customers will love.

luxury design

A luxury design is perfect for those who want to create a high-end and sophisticated restaurant. This style features the use of premium materials, intricate patterns, and ornate decor. The cost of implementing this design can range from ₹22 lakh to ₹25 lakh, depending on the complexity of the design and the materials used. This style suits restaurants that want to appeal to a affluent and discerning crowd.
