farmhouse villa interior design gurgaon mistakes — mistakes to avoid

july 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

farmhouse villa interior design Gurgaon: mistakes to avoid

not defining the scope of work

what goes wrong: without a clear scope of work, projects can quickly go off the rails, leading to cost overruns and delays. why it happens: homeowners often underestimate the complexity of their project or fail to communicate their vision effectively. exact fix: take the time to clearly define your scope of work, including all the elements you want to include in your design, and make sure to communicate it effectively to your designer.

tips for defining scope

  • start by making a list of all the rooms and spaces you want to design
  • consider your lifestyle and how you plan to use each space
  • research and gather inspiration to help clarify your vision

insufficient budgeting

what goes wrong: inadequate budgeting can lead to costly surprises down the line. why it happens: homeowners often underestimate the true cost of materials and labor. exact fix: work with your designer to create a detailed budget that takes into account all the elements of your design, including materials, labor, and any necessary permits or inspections.

as a general rule, it's a good idea to pad your budget by at least 10-20% to account for any unexpected expenses that may arise during the project.

poor space planning

what goes wrong: ineffective space planning can result in a design that's functional but lacking in aesthetic appeal. why it happens: homeowners often prioritize form over function, or vice versa. exact fix: work with your designer to create a space plan that balances both form and function, taking into account the flow of traffic, the placement of furniture and fixtures, and the overall ambiance you want to create.

considering sustainability

at indéva studio, we're committed to sustainable design practices, and we encourage our clients to consider the environmental impact of their design choices. you can learn more about sustainable design principles on the indian green building council website.

not researching local design trends

what goes wrong: a design that's out of touch with local trends and preferences can stick out like a sore thumb. why it happens: homeowners often fail to research local design trends and preferences. exact fix: take the time to research local design trends and preferences, and consider how they can be incorporated into your design. you can find inspiration in our portfolio of past projects, available on our portfolio page.

failing to communicate with your designer

what goes wrong: poor communication can lead to misunderstandings and costly mistakes. why it happens: homeowners often fail to communicate their vision and needs effectively to their designer. exact fix: make sure to communicate clearly and regularly with your designer, and don't be afraid to ask questions or seek clarification when needed. you can learn more about our design process and how we work with clients to bring their vision to life.
