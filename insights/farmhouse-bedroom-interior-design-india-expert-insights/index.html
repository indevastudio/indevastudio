why most farmhouse bedroom designs in india are a mistake

as a senior designer at indéva studio, i'm about to say something that might ruffle a few feathers: most farmhouse bedroom designs in india are a waste of space and money. with budgets ranging from ₹1 to 3 crore, it's astonishing how often we see poorly planned, inadequately functional, and just plain boring bedrooms in these luxury farmhouses.

the myth of traditional farmhouse design

indian homeowners often cling to traditional design elements, such as intricate woodwork and ornate furnishings, without considering how these features impact the overall functionality of the space. newsflash: just because it's traditional doesn't mean it's good design. in fact, many of these elements can make a room feel cluttered, dark, and downright uncomfortable.

a counterintuitive approach to luxury

so, what's the alternative? at indéva studio, we believe that true luxury lies in simplicity, not opulence. by stripping away unnecessary design elements and focusing on clean lines, minimal ornamentation, and an emphasis on natural light, we can create bedrooms that are not only beautiful but also functional and relaxing. and the best part? this approach can be achieved within a budget of ₹1 to 3 crore.

the importance of spatial planning

one of the most critical aspects of farmhouse bedroom design is spatial planning. a well-planned room can make all the difference in terms of functionality and comfort. here are a few tips to keep in mind:

  • consider the flow of traffic in the room and plan your furniture layout accordingly
  • use a combination of table and floor lamps to create a warm and inviting atmosphere
  • don't be afraid to think outside the box when it comes to storage solutions – a well-designed closet can be a game-changer

case study: a farmhouse in kundli, sonipat

we recently worked with a client in kundli, sonipat, who had a budget of ₹2.5 crore for their farmhouse bedroom design. by emphasizing natural materials, such as wood and stone, and incorporating plenty of greenery, we were able to create a space that felt both luxurious and grounded. the result was a bedroom that not only met but exceeded our client's expectations – and all within their budget.
