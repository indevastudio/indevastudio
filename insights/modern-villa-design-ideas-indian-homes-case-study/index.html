modern villa design ideas for indian homes: a case study

in a scenic area of kundli, sonipat, our client, mr. jain, had a vision of building a luxurious farmhouse that would serve as a weekend getaway for his family. with a budget of ₹2.5 crore, he approached indéva studio to bring his dream to life. our team of experts worked closely with mr. jain to understand his requirements and preferences, and the project timeline was set for 12 months, from february 2022 to february 2023.

the brief

the client's brief was to design a modern villa that would incorporate sustainable features, while also providing ample space for entertainment and relaxation. the farmhouse was to be built on a 2-acre plot, with a built-up area of approximately 10,000 square feet. our team at indéva studio worked tirelessly to create a design that would not only meet but exceed the client's expectations.

the challenge

one of the major challenges faced by our team was to design a villa that would be energy-efficient, while also incorporating the client's desire for large windows and open spaces. another challenge was to ensure that the design would blend seamlessly with the surrounding landscape. to overcome these challenges, our team conducted extensive research and consulted with experts in the field of sustainable design.

sustainable design features

some of the sustainable design features incorporated in the project include:

  • large windows and skylights to reduce the need for artificial lighting
  • insulation and double glazing to minimize heat gain and loss
  • rainwater harvesting and greywater reuse systems
  • use of natural and recycled materials for construction

the design solution

after careful consideration of the client's brief and the challenges faced, our team at indéva studio came up with a design solution that would meet the client's requirements while also incorporating sustainable features. the design included a large living area, dining room, kitchen, and five bedrooms, as well as a private movie theater, gym, and spa. the villa was designed to be energy-efficient, with a focus on natural ventilation and lighting.

the key to sustainable design is not to compromise on aesthetics, but to find innovative ways to incorporate eco-friendly features into the design. this not only reduces the environmental impact of the building but also provides a unique and luxurious living experience for the occupants.

the result

the final result was a stunning modern villa that exceeded the client's expectations. the villa was completed within the budget of ₹2.5 crore and was handed over to the client in february 2023. the client was thrilled with the outcome and praised our team at indéva studio for their professionalism and attention to detail. to learn more about our services and how we can help you with your design needs, please visit our website.
