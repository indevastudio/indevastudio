master bedroom interior design ideas india

most people approach master bedroom interior design backwards. here is the right sequence. when designing a master bedroom, especially in a builder floor in sector 44, noida, with a budget of ₹15–25 lakh, it's essential to start with the basics. what is the purpose of the room? what kind of furniture do you need? how much storage space do you require?

step 1: define the room's purpose and budget

what to do: start by defining the room's purpose and allocating a budget. for a master bedroom in sector 44, noida, you may want to allocate ₹5-10 lakh for furniture and fixtures, ₹3-5 lakh for flooring and walls, and ₹2-3 lakh for lighting and electrical work. what to ask your designer: what are the essential elements of a master bedroom? how can we optimize the space for maximum functionality? common pitfall: not allocating enough budget for storage and lighting.

step 2: choose the right furniture and layout

what to do: choose furniture that is both functional and aesthetically pleasing. consider a bed with storage, a dresser, and a wardrobe. what to ask your designer: what type of furniture would you recommend for a master bedroom? how can we create a layout that promotes relaxation and serenity? common pitfall: choosing furniture that is too large or too small for the room.

considering the 60-30-10 rule

  • 60% of the room should be a dominant color
  • 30% should be a secondary color
  • 10% should be an accent color
remember, the key to a well-designed master bedroom is to create a sense of balance and harmony. as the national institute of design would suggest, it's all about finding the perfect balance between form and function.

step 3: select the perfect materials and finishes

what to do: select materials and finishes that are both durable and aesthetically pleasing. consider using wooden flooring, painted walls, and a combination of table and floor lamps for lighting. what to ask your designer: what materials and finishes would you recommend for a master bedroom? how can we create a sense of warmth and coziness in the room? common pitfall: not considering the maintenance and upkeep of the materials and finishes.

step 4: add the final touches

what to do: add the final touches to the room, including curtains, rugs, and decorative accessories. what to ask your designer: what type of curtains and rugs would you recommend for a master bedroom? how can we add decorative accessories without overwhelming the space? common pitfall: not considering the overall aesthetic and style of the room when adding decorative accessories. you can learn more about our design process and our services to get a better understanding of how we can help you create your dream master bedroom.

step 5: review and revise

what to do: review the design plan and revise as necessary. what to ask your designer: what are the potential pitfalls of the design plan? how can we make adjustments to ensure that the room meets our needs and budget? common pitfall: not reviewing and revising the design plan before implementation. if you have any questions or need help with designing your master bedroom, get in touch with our designers at indéva studio to discuss your project.

frequently asked questions

what is the average cost of designing a master bedroom in india?

the average cost of designing a master bedroom in india can range from ₹10-50 lakh, depending on the size of the room, materials, and design elements.

how long does it take to design a master bedroom?

the design process for a master bedroom can take anywhere from 2-6 weeks, depending on the complexity of the design and the number of revisions required.

what are the most important elements of a master bedroom design?

the most important elements of a master bedroom design include a comfortable bed, adequate storage, and a sense of relaxation and serenity.

how can i ensure that my master bedroom design is functional and efficient?

you can ensure that your master bedroom design is functional and efficient by working with a professional designer who can help you create a layout that meets your needs and budget.
