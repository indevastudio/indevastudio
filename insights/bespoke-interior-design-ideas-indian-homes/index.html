bespoke interior design ideas for indian homes: a case study

our client, a family of four, had recently purchased a farmhouse in kundli, sonipat, with the intention of creating a luxurious and functional space for entertainment and relaxation. with a budget of ₹1–3 crore, they approached indéva studio to bring their vision to life.

the brief

the client's brief was to design a bespoke interior space that reflected their personal style and catered to their lifestyle. they wanted a spacious and airy feel, with ample natural light and ventilation. the farmhouse consisted of 5 bedrooms, a living room, dining room, kitchen, and outdoor spaces.

the challenge

one of the major challenges we faced was the client's requirement for a unique and customized design that would set their farmhouse apart from others in the region. they wanted to incorporate traditional indian elements with modern luxury, which posed a significant design challenge.

overcoming the challenge

to overcome this challenge, we conducted extensive research on traditional indian design elements and modern luxury trends. we also worked closely with the client to understand their personal preferences and tastes. based on our research and client feedback, we decided to incorporate traditional indian materials and craftsmanship, such as handmade furniture and intricate woodwork, with modern luxury elements, such as smart home technology and high-end fixtures.

the design solution

our design solution was to create a bespoke interior space that blended traditional indian elements with modern luxury. we started by selecting a neutral color palette and incorporating natural materials, such as wood and stone, to create a warm and inviting atmosphere. we then added modern luxury elements, such as smart home technology and high-end fixtures, to create a functional and efficient space.

  • we designed a custom-made wooden door with intricate carvings to serve as the main entrance
  • we incorporated handmade furniture pieces, such as a wooden sofa and armchairs, to add a touch of traditional indian craftsmanship
  • we installed smart home technology to control lighting, temperature, and security systems
the key to creating a successful bespoke interior design is to understand the client's personal style and preferences, and to incorporate those elements into the design. in this case, the client's love of traditional indian craftsmanship and modern luxury helped to guide our design decisions.

the result

the final result was a luxurious and functional space that reflected the client's personal style and catered to their lifestyle. the bespoke interior design solution we provided helped to create a unique and inviting atmosphere, with ample natural light and ventilation. the client was thrilled with the final result, and the project was completed within the budget of ₹2.5 crore.
