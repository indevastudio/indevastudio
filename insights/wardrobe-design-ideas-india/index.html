july 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

the surprising truth about wardrobe design costs in india

when it comes to designing a wardrobe for your bedroom in india, the costs can quickly add up, with many homeowners in south delhi, delhi ending up spending upwards of ₹20 lakh. however, the biggest cost misconception in india is that custom wardrobe design has to be expensive, with many designers charging upwards of ₹5 lakh for a single unit. but what if you could get a beautifully designed wardrobe for your bedroom for a fraction of the cost?

what drives costs up in wardrobe design

there are several factors that can drive up the cost of wardrobe design in india, including the type of materials used, the complexity of the design, and the size of the unit. for example, a wardrobe design that includes premium materials like solid wood or glass can cost upwards of ₹3 lakh, while a more basic design using mdf or plywood can cost significantly less, around ₹1.5 lakh.

cost breakdown by room

when it comes to designing a wardrobe for your bedroom, the cost can vary depending on the size of the room and the type of design you choose. for a small bedroom in south delhi, delhi, a basic wardrobe design can cost around ₹1 lakh, while a more premium design can cost upwards of ₹2.5 lakh. for a larger bedroom, the cost can be significantly higher, with prices ranging from ₹2 lakh to ₹5 lakh or more.

breakdown of costs for a typical bedroom wardrobe design project

  • design and planning: ₹50,000 - ₹1 lakh
  • materials and fabrication: ₹1 lakh - ₹2.5 lakh
  • installation and labor: ₹50,000 - ₹1 lakh
  • total: ₹2 lakh - ₹4.5 lakh

how to negotiate with your designer

when working with a designer to create a custom wardrobe for your bedroom, it's essential to negotiate the cost to ensure you get the best value for your money. one way to do this is to provide your designer with a clear budget and ask them to work within it. you can also ask for a detailed breakdown of the costs to understand where your money is going. as indéva studio's designers always say,

the key to a successful design project is clear communication and a mutual understanding of the budget and scope of work

red flags that inflate bills

there are several red flags to watch out for when working with a designer to create a custom wardrobe for your bedroom, including vague or unclear pricing, a lack of transparency in the design process, and unexpected changes to the scope of work. to avoid these issues, it's essential to work with a reputable designer who is transparent about their costs and process. you can also check out resources like architectural digest india for inspiration and guidance on designing your dream bedroom.
