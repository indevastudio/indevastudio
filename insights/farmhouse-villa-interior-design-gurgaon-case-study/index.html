farmhouse villa interior design Gurgaon: a case study

in the heart of delhi, a young family of four was looking to create a dream home in their newly purchased duplex in greater kailash. with a budget of ₹40-80 lakh, they wanted a space that reflected their love for luxury and comfort. after researching online and reading reviews, they decided to consult with indéva studio, a renowned interior design firm in delhi ncr.

the brief

the client's brief was clear: they wanted a farmhouse villa interior design that would make their duplex feel like a luxurious retreat. they provided us with a timeline of 12 weeks and a budget of ₹60 lakh. our team at indéva studio was excited to take on the challenge and create a bespoke design that would meet the client's expectations.

the challenge

the biggest challenge we faced was the tight timeline and budget. the client wanted high-quality materials and finishes, but we had to ensure that we stayed within the budget. our team conducted extensive research and sourced materials from local suppliers to keep costs under control. we also worked closely with the client to prioritize their needs and make decisions quickly.

key considerations

  • creating a functional layout that met the client's needs
  • selecting materials and finishes that were within budget
  • ensuring that the design was consistent with the client's vision
our design approach is centered around understanding the client's lifestyle and preferences. we believe that a well-designed space should not only be aesthetically pleasing but also functional and comfortable.

the design solution

our team at indéva studio developed a comprehensive design plan that addressed the client's needs and budget. we created a bespoke design that incorporated the client's love for luxury and comfort. the final design included high-quality materials, such as italian marble and engineered wood, and a functional layout that met the client's needs.

design decisions

we decided to use a neutral color palette with accents of warm colors to create a cozy and inviting atmosphere. we also incorporated natural textures, such as wood and stone, to add depth and visual interest to the space. our team worked closely with the client to select furniture and lighting that was both functional and stylish.

the result

the final outcome was stunning. the client was thrilled with the result, and the project was completed within the timeline and budget. the total cost of the project was ₹58 lakh, which was within the client's budget of ₹60 lakh. the client was impressed with the attention to detail and the quality of the workmanship.
