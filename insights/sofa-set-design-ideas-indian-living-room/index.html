why traditional sofa set designs are holding back your indian living room

most designers won't tell you this, but the traditional sofa set designs that dominate indian living rooms are often a safe, yet unfulfilling choice, and it's time to break free from these constraints, especially when working with a luxurious budget of ₹1-3 crore in a penthouse in Sector 150, Noida.

the myth of the "perfect" sofa set

for years, indian homeowners have been led to believe that a traditional sofa set is the key to a well-designed living room, but this couldn't be further from the truth, as it often leads to a lack of personality and character in the space, which is why at indéva studio, we encourage our clients to think outside the box and explore unique design ideas that reflect their individuality.

counterintuitive insights for indian living rooms

one counterintuitive insight that can help you create a more luxurious and inviting living room is to focus on negative space, rather than trying to fill every corner with furniture, which can make the space feel cluttered and overwhelming, and with a budget of ₹2 crore, you can afford to invest in high-quality, minimalist pieces that create a sense of calm and serenity.

the power of negative space

by embracing negative space, you can create a sense of flow and visual balance in your living room, which can be particularly challenging in indian homes, where the tendency is to over-accessorize and over-furnish, but with the right guidance, such as that provided by architectural digest india, you can learn to appreciate the beauty of restraint and minimalism.

  • use a statement piece of art or sculpture to create a focal point in the room
  • invest in a high-quality, minimalist sofa that provides ample seating without overwhelming the space
  • add texture and depth with throw pillows, blankets, and rugs, but avoid over-accessorizing
the key to creating a truly luxurious living room is not to fill it with expensive furniture and decor, but to create a sense of space and freedom, which can be achieved with a budget of ₹1-3 crore, and a willingness to think outside the box and challenge traditional design conventions.

frequently asked questions

what is the best way to choose a sofa set for my indian living room?

the best way to choose a sofa set is to consider the specific needs and preferences of your family, as well as the overall aesthetic you want to create in your living room, and to work with a experienced designer who can guide you through the process and help you make informed decisions.

how much should i budget for a sofa set in my ₹2 crore living room design project?

a good rule of thumb is to allocate 10-20% of your total budget to the sofa set, which would be ₹20-40 lakh for a ₹2 crore project, but the key is to find a piece that reflects your personal style and meets your needs, rather than just focusing on the price tag.

can i use a traditional sofa set in a modern indian living room?

while it's possible to use a traditional sofa set in a modern indian living room, it's often challenging to make it work, as traditional sofa sets can feel heavy and overwhelming in a modern space, which is why we recommend experimenting with more modern and minimalist designs that can provide a sense of contrast and visual interest.
