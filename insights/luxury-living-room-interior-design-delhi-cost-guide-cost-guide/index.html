luxury living room interior design delhi cost guide — cost & budget guide

july 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio
photo by Lotus Design N Print on unsplash

the surprising truth about luxury living room interior design costs in delhi

when it comes to designing a luxury living room in delhi, many homeowners assume that the cost will be exorbitant, often exceeding ₹20-30 lakh. however, with careful planning and smart design choices, it's possible to achieve a luxurious look within a budget of ₹8-15 lakh, perfect for a weekend retreat in KMP Expressway, Sonipat.

what drives costs up in luxury living room interior design

several factors contribute to the overall cost of a luxury living room design, including the choice of materials, furniture, and lighting. for instance, using high-end materials like italian marble or exotic wood can increase costs significantly, with prices ranging from ₹500 to ₹2,000 per square foot. on the other hand, opting for more affordable alternatives like engineered wood or local marble can help stay within the ₹8-15 lakh budget.

cost breakdown by room: a detailed analysis

to get a better understanding of the costs involved, let's break down the expenses for a typical luxury living room in delhi. here's a rough estimate of the costs:

  • flooring: ₹1.5-3 lakh
  • walls and ceiling: ₹1-2 lakh
  • lighting: ₹50,000-1 lakh
  • furniture: ₹2-5 lakh
  • decor and accessories: ₹50,000-1 lakh

how to allocate your ₹8-15 lakh budget effectively

with a clear understanding of the costs involved, it's essential to allocate your budget wisely. consider prioritizing the most critical elements, such as flooring and furniture, and then allocate the remaining budget to other aspects like lighting and decor.

how to negotiate with contractors and designers

negotiating with contractors and designers can be challenging, but it's crucial to get the best value for your money. here's a tip:

instead of asking for a discount, ask the contractor or designer to provide a detailed breakdown of the costs involved. this will help you identify areas where you can cut back and allocate the budget more efficiently.

red flags that inflate bills: what to watch out for

when working with contractors or designers, it's essential to be aware of potential red flags that can inflate your bills. some common culprits include:

  • hidden costs or unexpected expenses
  • low-quality materials or workmanship
  • poor communication or project management

real project budget example: a luxury living room in KMP Expressway, Sonipat

let's take a look at a real project example: a luxury living room design for a weekend retreat in KMP Expressway, Sonipat, with a budget of ₹12 lakh. here's how the budget was allocated:

after consulting with indéva studio's designers, the homeowner decided to allocate the budget as follows: ₹2 lakh for flooring, ₹1.5 lakh for walls and ceiling, ₹75,000 for lighting, ₹4 lakh for furniture, and ₹1 lakh for decor and accessories. the result was a stunning luxury living room that exceeded the homeowner's expectations, all within the budget of ₹12 lakh.

frequently asked questions

what is the average cost of a luxury living room design in delhi?

the average cost of a luxury living room design in delhi can range from ₹8-20 lakh, depending on the size of the room, materials used, and design complexity.

how long does it take to complete a luxury living room design project?

the duration of a luxury living room design project can vary from 6-12 weeks, depending on the complexity of the design and the number of stakeholders involved.

what are the most important factors to consider when designing a luxury living room?

when designing a luxury living room, it's essential to consider factors such as the room's purpose, the homeowner's lifestyle, and the overall aesthetic and ambiance desired.

how can i ensure that my luxury living room design stays within budget?

to ensure that your luxury living room design stays within budget, it's crucial to work with a reputable designer or contractor, prioritize your spending, and regularly monitor expenses.
