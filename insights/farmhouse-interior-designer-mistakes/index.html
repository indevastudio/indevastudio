avoiding costly mistakes in farmhouse interior design

insufficient planning and budgeting

What goes wrong: insufficient planning and budgeting can lead to costly changes down the line, blowing your budget of ₹40-80 lakh. Why it happens: many designers and clients skip this crucial step, eager to dive into the design process. Exact fix: take the time to create a comprehensive plan and budget, considering all aspects of the project, from materials to labor costs.

the importance of a detailed budget

  • allocate funds for each aspect of the project
  • consider contingencies and unexpected expenses
  • regularly review and adjust the budget as needed

poor communication and lack of transparency

What goes wrong: poor communication and lack of transparency can lead to misunderstandings and errors. Why it happens: designers and clients often have different expectations and communication styles. Exact fix: establish clear and open communication channels, ensuring that all parties are on the same page throughout the project.

at indéva studio, we believe that transparency is key to a successful design project. Our clients have access to a dedicated project manager, ensuring that their concerns and questions are always addressed.

inadequate attention to spatial planning

What goes wrong: inadequate attention to spatial planning can result in a space that is functional but lacking in aesthetic appeal. Why it happens: designers may prioritize style over substance, neglecting the importance of spatial planning. Exact fix: work with a designer who understands the importance of balancing form and function, creating a space that is both beautiful and functional.

the benefits of good spatial planning

  • optimizes the use of space
  • enhances the overall aesthetic appeal of the space
  • improves functionality and usability
