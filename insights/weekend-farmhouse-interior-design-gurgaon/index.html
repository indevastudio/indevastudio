weekend farmhouse interior design Gurgaon: a case study

in the heart of Kundli, Sonipat, our client, a family of four, had always dreamed of owning a weekend farmhouse where they could escape the chaos of city life and connect with nature. with a budget of ₹1-3 crore, they approached indéva studio to bring their vision to life. our team was excited to take on the challenge and create a serene and luxurious space that reflected their love for nature and entertainment.

the brief

the client's brief was clear: they wanted a farmhouse that would serve as a weekend getaway, where they could host friends and family, and enjoy the serene surroundings. they had purchased a 2-acre plot in Kundli, Sonipat, and wanted us to design the interior and exterior spaces to perfection. our team began by conducting a site analysis, studying the topography, climate, and natural surroundings to ensure that our design would be in harmony with the environment.

the challenge

one of the major challenges we faced was the tight timeline. the client wanted the project to be completed within 6 months, which meant that we had to work efficiently and effectively to meet the deadline. another challenge was the budget, which was ₹2.5 crore, leaving us with limited room for experimentation. however, our team was determined to deliver a high-quality design that met the client's expectations.

key considerations

  • site analysis and climate study
  • budget allocation and cost management
  • timeline management and project scheduling

the design solution

our design solution was centered around creating a harmonious balance between nature and luxury. we designed the farmhouse with large windows and sliding doors that opened up to the surrounding landscape, bringing the outdoors in. the interior spaces were designed to be warm and inviting, with a mix of natural materials such as wood, stone, and brick. we also incorporated sustainable design elements, such as solar panels and a rainwater harvesting system, to reduce the carbon footprint of the farmhouse.

as designers, we believe that the true beauty of a space lies in its ability to connect us with nature and ourselves. our goal was to create a farmhouse that would not only be a luxurious retreat but also a sustainable and responsible one.

the result

the final result was a stunning farmhouse that exceeded the client's expectations. the interior spaces were designed to perfection, with a mix of modern and traditional elements that reflected the client's personal style. the exterior spaces were equally impressive, with a beautifully landscaped garden and a large outdoor pool. the client was thrilled with the final result, and we were proud to have delivered a project that met their budget of ₹2.5 crore.
