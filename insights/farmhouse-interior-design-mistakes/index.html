farmhouse interior design ideas india: mistakes to avoid

the day a client called us mid-project, frantic about the ₹50 lakh they had already spent on their luxury apartment in dlf phase 5, gurgaon, is a day that still resonates with our team at indéva studio. the client had chosen a designer who promised a "unique" and "custom" look, but what they got was a space that was neither functional nor beautiful. as we worked to fix the mistakes and bring the project back on track, we realized that this was not an isolated incident - many homeowners in india make similar mistakes when it comes to designing their farmhouses, often with devastating consequences.

incorrect lighting plan

one of the most common mistakes in farmhouse interior design is an incorrect lighting plan. this can lead to a space that is either too dark or too bright, making it uncomfortable to inhabit. the reason this happens is that many designers and homeowners focus too much on the aesthetic appeal of lighting fixtures, rather than their functionality. to fix this, it's essential to create a comprehensive lighting plan that takes into account the natural light available, the activities that will take place in each room, and the overall ambiance you want to create.

types of lighting

  • task lighting: for areas where specific tasks will be performed, such as reading or cooking
  • ambient lighting: for creating a general glow and setting the mood
  • accent lighting: for highlighting specific features or objects
lighting is not just about illuminating a space - it's about creating an atmosphere and setting the tone for the entire room. by choosing the right type and amount of lighting, you can completely transform the look and feel of your farmhouse.

inadequate storage

another mistake that many homeowners make is not providing adequate storage in their farmhouses. this can lead to clutter and disorganization, making the space feel cramped and uncomfortable. the reason this happens is that many designers and homeowners focus too much on the visual appeal of a room, rather than its functionality. to fix this, it's essential to incorporate plenty of storage options, such as closets, cabinets, and shelves, into your design plan. for example, a client in dlf phase 5, gurgaon, recently spent ₹60 lakh on a luxury apartment, only to realize that they had not provided enough storage for their belongings.

poor space planning

poor space planning is another common mistake in farmhouse interior design. this can lead to a space that feels cramped, cluttered, and uncomfortable. the reason this happens is that many designers and homeowners do not take the time to carefully consider the flow and functionality of each room. to fix this, it's essential to create a comprehensive space plan that takes into account the activities that will take place in each room, the furniture and fixtures that will be used, and the overall flow and circulation of the space. for instance, a well-planned space can help you save up to ₹20 lakh in furniture and decor costs.

ignoring the importance of materials

ignoring the importance of materials is another mistake that many homeowners make when designing their farmhouses. this can lead to a space that feels cheap, flimsy, and uninviting. the reason this happens is that many designers and homeowners focus too much on the aesthetic appeal of materials, rather than their quality and durability. to fix this, it's essential to choose materials that are not only beautiful but also functional and long-lasting. for example, using high-quality wood or stone can add up to ₹15 lakh to the cost of your project, but it can also increase the value of your property.

failing to consider the indian climate

failing to consider the indian climate is another mistake that many homeowners make when designing their farmhouses. this can lead to a space that is either too hot or too cold, making it uncomfortable to inhabit. the reason this happens is that many designers and homeowners do not take the time to carefully consider the local climate and how it will impact their design. to fix this, it's essential to incorporate design elements that take into account the indian climate, such as large windows for natural ventilation, insulated roofs, and cooling systems. according to the national institute of design, a well-designed space can reduce energy costs by up to 30%.
