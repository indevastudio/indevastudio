Material Comparison for Delhi NCR Homes

One of the most common misconceptions about HDHMR and marine plywood is that they are interchangeable terms, but in reality, they have distinct differences in terms of material properties, cost, and durability. As a senior writer for indéva studio, a Delhi NCR luxury interior design firm, I will break down the key differences between these two materials to help homeowners make an informed decision for their home in Delhi NCR.

Material Properties

HDHMR (High Density High Moisture Resistant) is a type of engineered wood that is designed to withstand high moisture levels, making it an ideal choice for kitchens and bathrooms. On the other hand, marine plywood is a type of plywood that is specifically designed for use in marine environments, where it is exposed to high levels of moisture and saltwater. While both materials have their own strengths, HDHMR is generally more resistant to moisture and termites, making it a popular choice for Delhi NCR homes.

Cost Difference

The cost of HDHMR and marine plywood can vary depending on the specific product and manufacturer. However, in general, HDHMR tends to be more expensive than marine plywood, with prices ranging from ₹500 to ₹1,500 per sheet, depending on the thickness and quality. For a home in Delhi NCR with a budget of ₹1–3 crore, the cost of materials can add up quickly, making it essential to choose the right material for the job.

Illustrative Budget

For example, if you're building a home in Delhi NCR with a budget of ₹2 crore, you may need to allocate ₹5-10 lakh for materials, depending on the size and complexity of the project. In this case, choosing the right material can make a significant difference in the overall cost of the project.

Durability and Moisture Resistance

Both HDHMR and marine plywood are designed to withstand high levels of moisture, but they have different levels of resistance. HDHMR is generally more resistant to moisture and termites, making it a popular choice for kitchens and bathrooms. Marine plywood, on the other hand, is specifically designed for use in marine environments and is more resistant to saltwater and extreme weather conditions.

Where Each Option Makes Sense

HDHMR is a great choice for interior design projects in Delhi NCR, particularly for kitchens and bathrooms, where moisture levels are high. Marine plywood, on the other hand, is better suited for outdoor projects, such as building a deck or a boat. At indéva studio, we specify HDHMR by default for most of our interior design projects, due to its high moisture resistance and durability.

As a designer, it's essential to consider the specific needs of each project and choose the material that best fits the requirements. While HDHMR and marine plywood have their own strengths, it's crucial to weigh the pros and cons of each material before making a decision.
