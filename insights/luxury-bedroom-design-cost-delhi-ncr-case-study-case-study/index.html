luxury bedroom design cost delhi ncr: a case study

in the summer of 2022, we received a call from a client who owned a beautiful farmhouse in kundli, sonipat, and was looking to renovate the master bedroom. the client had a budget of ₹1–3 crore and wanted to create a luxurious and relaxing space that reflected their personal style.

the brief

the client's brief was clear: they wanted a luxury bedroom design that would make them feel like they were in a 5-star hotel. they had a specific vision in mind, which included a large walk-in closet, a spacious bedroom with a sitting area, and an en-suite bathroom with a separate shower and bathtub. they also wanted to incorporate sustainable design elements, as they were interested in reducing their carbon footprint. we discussed the possibility of obtaining a green building certification from the indian green building council, which would not only reduce their environmental impact but also increase the value of their property.

client's requirements

  • large walk-in closet with custom storage solutions
  • spacious bedroom with a sitting area and a large window with a view of the surrounding landscape
  • en-suite bathroom with a separate shower and bathtub
  • sustainable design elements, including energy-efficient lighting and solar panels

the challenge

the challenge was to create a luxury bedroom design that met the client's requirements while staying within their budget of ₹1–3 crore. we had to carefully consider every aspect of the design, from the materials used to the layout of the space. we also had to ensure that the design was sustainable and environmentally friendly, which added an extra layer of complexity to the project.

the key to a successful luxury bedroom design is to create a space that is both beautiful and functional. it's not just about throwing money at the problem, but about making smart design decisions that meet the client's needs and budget.

the design solution

after several meetings with the client, we came up with a design solution that met their requirements and stayed within their budget. we decided to use a combination of materials, including wood, stone, and glass, to create a luxurious and sustainable space. we also incorporated energy-efficient lighting and solar panels to reduce the client's carbon footprint. the final design included a large walk-in closet with custom storage solutions, a spacious bedroom with a sitting area, and an en-suite bathroom with a separate shower and bathtub.

design decisions

  • using reclaimed wood for the closet and bedroom furniture to reduce waste and add a touch of warmth to the space
  • incorporating energy-efficient lighting, including led bulbs and solar-powered lamps
  • using low-flow showerheads and toilets to reduce water consumption

the result

the final result was a stunning luxury bedroom design that exceeded the client's expectations. the space was not only beautiful but also functional and sustainable. the client was thrilled with the final product, and we were proud to have delivered a project that met their needs and budget. the final cost of the project was ₹2.5 crore, which was within the client's budget of ₹1–3 crore.
