premium interior designers delhi ncr: a cost guide

one of the biggest cost misconceptions in india is that hiring a premium interior designer will always exceed your budget, but with a clear understanding of the costs involved, you can plan your dream weekend retreat in KMP Expressway, Sonipat with a budget of ₹8-15 lakh, and still achieve the luxury look you desire.

what drives costs up in interior design projects

several factors contribute to the overall cost of an interior design project, including the size of the space, the materials and finishes chosen, and the complexity of the design, with costs ranging from ₹1,500 to ₹3,500 per square foot, depending on the level of luxury and customization.

cost breakdown by room

when planning your weekend retreat, it's essential to consider the cost of each room, with costs varying from ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh per room, depending on the size and level of design, for example:

  • living room: ₹2-4 lakh
  • dining room: ₹1-2 lakh
  • bedroom: ₹1.5-3 lakh

cost of materials and finishes

the cost of materials and finishes can significantly impact the overall cost of your project, with prices ranging from ₹500 to ₹5,000 per square foot, depending on the quality and brand, for example, a high-end wooden floor can cost ₹2,500 per square foot, while a mid-range option can cost ₹1,200 per square foot.

when it comes to materials and finishes, it's not just about the cost, but also about the durability and maintenance requirements, as a more expensive option may be more cost-effective in the long run.

how to negotiate with your interior designer

negotiating with your interior designer can help you stay within your budget, by discussing the scope of work, the materials and finishes, and the timeline, for example, you can ask your designer to:

  • provide a detailed breakdown of the costs involved
  • offer alternative materials and finishes to reduce costs
  • phase the project to prioritize the most essential elements

red flags that inflate bills

certain red flags can inflate your bill, including:

  • unexpected site visits or meetings
  • changes to the original scope of work
  • high-end materials or finishes that exceed your budget
it's essential to discuss these potential issues with your designer upfront to avoid surprises.
