plotted home interior design sonipat: a haven of luxury and tranquility

Imagine stepping into a plush farmhouse in Kundli, Sonipat, where the warmth of natural light pours in through the large windows, illuminating the sleek, modern lines of the interior. The space is adorned with intricate Indian patterns and motifs, blending seamlessly with the lush greenery outside, creating a perfect harmony of luxury and nature. With a budget of ₹1-3 crore, the possibilities are endless, and indéva studio is here to guide you through the process of creating your dream home.

modern farmhouse design

A modern farmhouse design is perfect for those who want to experience the best of luxury living in the midst of nature. This style features clean lines, minimal ornamentation, and an emphasis on functionality. Materials such as steel, glass, and concrete are commonly used, along with natural elements like wood and stone. The cost range for this style can vary from ₹1.5-2.5 crore, depending on the scale and complexity of the design. This style suits individuals who value simplicity, elegance, and a strong connection with the outdoors.

traditional indian design

For those who want to incorporate traditional Indian elements into their plotted home interior design, this style is an excellent choice. It features intricate patterns, ornate details, and a rich use of colors and textures. Materials such as wood, marble, and intricate tile work are commonly used, along with traditional Indian fabrics and textiles. The cost range for this style can vary from ₹2-3 crore, depending on the level of intricacy and craftsmanship. This style suits individuals who value cultural heritage, tradition, and a sense of warmth and hospitality.

incorporating local elements

One of the key aspects of traditional Indian design is the incorporation of local elements, such as intricate carvings, ornate furniture, and vibrant textiles. These elements can add a unique touch to your plotted home interior design, making it truly special and reflective of the local culture. Some popular local elements in Kundli, Sonipat, include:

  • Intricate wooden carvings and furniture
  • Ornate metalwork and decorative items
  • Vibrant textiles and traditional Indian fabrics
When incorporating local elements, it's essential to strike a balance between tradition and modernity, creating a space that is both culturally rich and functional, as seen in the designs featured on elle decor india

luxury minimalism

Luxury minimalism is a design style that combines the simplicity of minimalism with the opulence of luxury. It features clean lines, minimal ornamentation, and a focus on high-quality materials and finishes. This style is perfect for those who value elegance, sophistication, and a sense of calm. The cost range for this style can vary from ₹1.2-2.2 crore, depending on the scale and complexity of the design. This style suits individuals who value simplicity, elegance, and a strong connection with the outdoors.

fusion design

A fusion design combines elements from different styles, creating a unique and eclectic space that reflects the individual's personality. This style features a mix of modern and traditional elements, along with a blend of different materials and textures. The cost range for this style can vary from ₹1.5-2.5 crore, depending on the level of complexity and customization. This style suits individuals who value creativity, individuality, and a sense of adventure.
