high end cafe and restaurant design delhi: a case study

in a bustling city like delhi, finding the perfect spot to relax and enjoy a cup of coffee or a meal can be a challenge. for one of our clients, a young entrepreneur, this challenge became an opportunity to create a unique and luxurious cafe experience in the heart of greater kailash, delhi. with a budget of ₹40-80 lakh and a duplex space to work with, our team at indéva studio was tasked with designing a high-end cafe and restaurant that would stand out from the crowd.

the brief

our client's vision was to create a sophisticated and elegant space that would appeal to delhi's affluent crowd. the brief included designing a duplex cafe and restaurant with a total area of 2000 square feet, including a dining area, lounge, kitchen, and restrooms. the client wanted to incorporate premium materials and finishes, such as marble, wood, and metal, to create a luxurious ambiance.

understanding the client's needs

to better understand our client's needs and preferences, we conducted a series of meetings and discussions. we also visited the site to assess the space and identify potential challenges. our team worked closely with the client to develop a detailed design brief, which included the following requirements:

  • create a luxurious and elegant atmosphere
  • incorporate premium materials and finishes
  • design a functional and efficient kitchen and dining area
  • ensure adequate lighting and ventilation

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges we faced was working within the given budget of ₹40-80 lakh. to stay within budget, we had to make careful design decisions and select materials and finishes that were not only luxurious but also cost-effective. another challenge was designing a functional and efficient kitchen and dining area, while also creating a luxurious ambiance.

as designers, we have to balance the client's vision with the practicalities of the project. in this case, we had to ensure that the design was not only aesthetically pleasing but also functional and efficient.

the design solution

our design solution included creating a luxurious and elegant atmosphere by incorporating premium materials and finishes, such as marble, wood, and metal. we designed a functional and efficient kitchen and dining area, with adequate lighting and ventilation. to stay within budget, we selected materials and finishes that were not only luxurious but also cost-effective.

design decisions

some of the key design decisions we made included:

  • using marble flooring and cladding to create a luxurious ambiance
  • incorporating wood and metal accents to add warmth and texture
  • designing a functional and efficient kitchen with high-end appliances
  • creating a lounge area with comfortable seating and adequate lighting

the result

the final result was a luxurious and elegant cafe and restaurant that exceeded our client's expectations. the total cost of the project came in at ₹60 lakh, which was within the client's budget. the client was thrilled with the outcome and has reported a significant increase in business since the cafe and restaurant opened.
