weekend farmhouse interior design gurgaon costs — cost & budget guide

august 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

the surprising truth about weekend farmhouse interior design costs in gurgaon

when it comes to designing a weekend farmhouse in kundli, sonipat, many of us assume that the biggest expense will be the land purchase or construction costs. however, the reality is that interior design can often account for a significant chunk of the overall budget, with some farmhouses in the ₹1-3 crore range allocate up to ₹50-75 lakhs for interior design alone.

what drives up the costs of weekend farmhouse interior design in gurgaon

several factors can contribute to the high costs of interior design for a weekend farmhouse in gurgaon, including the use of high-end materials, custom furniture, and intricate design details. for instance, a single bespoke piece of furniture can cost anywhere from ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakhs, depending on the design, materials, and craftsmanship.

cost breakdown by room for a weekend farmhouse in kundli, sonipat

to give you a better idea of what to expect, here's a rough breakdown of the costs for each room in a weekend farmhouse:

  • living room: ₹8-12 lakhs (including furniture, lighting, and decor)
  • kitchen: ₹10-15 lakhs (including cabinets, countertops, and appliances)
  • bedrooms: ₹5-8 lakhs each (including furniture, lighting, and decor)
  • bathrooms: ₹3-5 lakhs each (including fixtures, tiles, and vanities)

how to negotiate with your interior designer to stay within budget

working with an experienced interior designer can help you stay within your budget, but it's essential to communicate your expectations clearly. be sure to discuss your budget upfront and ask for a detailed breakdown of the costs. you can also consider phasing the design project to prioritize the most essential elements.

as a designer, i always tell my clients that it's better to invest in a few high-quality, timeless pieces rather than trying to fill the space with cheaper, trendy items that may need to be replaced soon. - indéva studio designer

red flags that can inflate your weekend farmhouse interior design bills

be wary of designers who:

  • fail to provide a clear breakdown of the costs
  • push you to purchase expensive materials or products
  • don't have a clear plan for the project timeline and budget
