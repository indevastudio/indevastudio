delhi luxury home interior cost guide: budgeting custom furniture

september 2026 · 5 min read · indéva studio

Most homeowners assume a high‑end interior will cost over ₹30 lakh, but data from indéva studio shows the average luxury remodel in Delhi NCR stays between ₹8 and ₹15 lakh when smart choices are made.

The biggest cost myth in Indian luxury interiors

In India, the prevailing belief is that “luxury equals extravagance.” This myth leads buyers to over‑budget before a single sketch is drawn. The truth is that the largest expense often comes from a lack of planning rather than from premium materials. When a project starts with a clear budget framework, the same level of elegance can be achieved at a fraction of the perceived price.

What drives costs up in a Delhi home

Understanding the cost drivers helps homeowners make informed decisions. Below are the primary factors that can push a project beyond the ₹8–15 lakh range.

  • Material selection: Imported marble or rare hardwoods carry high freight and duty costs.
  • Custom furniture: Bespoke pieces designed from scratch are labor‑intensive and can double the price of off‑the‑shelf alternatives.
  • Design revisions: Each change after the design freeze adds consultancy fees and re‑work charges.
  • Installation complexity: Tight urban sites in Delhi NCR often require extra manpower and equipment.
  • Finishing details: Hand‑crafted joinery, specialty lighting, and premium hardware increase the final bill.

Cost breakdown by room

Allocating the budget room by room provides a realistic roadmap. The following percentages are based on recent projects across Delhi NCR, adjusted for a total spend of ₹12 lakh – a midpoint in the illustrative range.

Living room – 30% (≈ ₹3.6 lakh)

Key items include a statement sofa set, coffee table, and built‑in media wall. Opt for a mix of ready‑made sofas and one or two furniture custom accent pieces to keep costs balanced.

Master bedroom – 25% (≈ ₹3 lakh)

Focus on a solid platform bed, wardrobe, and bedside tables. A custom furniture manufacturer Delhi can create a wardrobe that maximizes storage while using locally sourced wood to stay within budget.

Kitchen – 20% (≈ ₹2.4 lakh)

Modular cabinets, quartz countertops, and integrated appliances dominate this segment. Choosing standard cabinet dimensions and adding a few Delhi NCR furniture accessories, like a custom island, yields a high‑end look without overspending.

Bathrooms – 10% (≈ ₹1.2 lakh)

Tile selection and sanitary ware are the main cost drivers. Use large‑format tiles to reduce grout lines and labor time, and consider prefabricated vanity units that can be refinished.

Hallways & entry – 5% (≈ ₹0.6 lakh)

Simple wall panels, a console table, and statement lighting create a welcoming feel. These are ideal spots for furniture custom pieces that showcase craftsmanship without a large price tag.

Contingency – 10% (≈ ₹1.2 lakh)

Always reserve a buffer for unexpected expenses such as site conditions or last‑minute design tweaks.

How to negotiate with a custom furniture manufacturer Delhi

Negotiation is an art, not a battle. Below are steps to secure the best value from a reputable maker.

  • Define scope early: Provide detailed drawings and material preferences before the first quote.
  • Ask for itemised pricing: Break down labor, material, and finish costs to spot hidden mark‑ups.
  • Leverage volume: Ordering multiple pieces (e.g., a bedroom set) often unlocks discounts.
  • Consider phased delivery: Staggered shipments reduce storage fees and allow cash‑flow management.
  • Request sample finishes: Seeing a physical sample prevents costly re‑finishing later.
“The most effective way to lower the bill is not to cut corners, but to cut ambiguity. Clear specifications turn design dreams into predictable costs.”

Red flags that inflate your bill

Even with a solid plan, certain practices can silently raise expenses. Watch out for these warning signs.

  • Vague contracts that lack a clear change‑order clause.
  • Unexplained “site supervision fees” that appear after work begins.
  • Material substitutions without prior approval.
  • Repeated design revisions after the freeze date.
  • Unlicensed subcontractors handling electrical or plumbing work.

When any of these appear, pause the project and request a written clarification before proceeding.

frequently asked questions

Q1? How much does a single custom sofa cost in Delhi NCR?

A typical high‑end custom sofa ranges from ₹80,000 to ₹2,00,000 depending on upholstery, frame material, and detailing.

Q2? Can I mix ready‑made and custom pieces without compromising style?

Yes. Pairing a ready‑made sofa with a custom coffee table or sideboard creates visual interest while controlling costs.

Q3? What is the best way to verify the quality of wood used in custom furniture?

Ask for a wood sample and check for certification such as FSC. Inspect grain consistency and moisture content (ideally 6‑8%).

Q4? How long does it take to manufacture and install custom furniture for a 2‑bedroom unit?

From final design approval to installation, the timeline is usually 8‑12 weeks, depending on material availability and site access.
