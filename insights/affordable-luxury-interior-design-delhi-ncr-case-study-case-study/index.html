affordable luxury interior design delhi ncr: a case study

mr. and mrs. jain, a young couple, had recently purchased a plotted home in rai, sonipat, with the dream of turning it into their perfect luxury abode. however, with a budget of ₹25–50 lakh, they were unsure if they could achieve the luxury interior design they desired. that's when they approached indéva studio, a delhi ncr-based luxury interior design firm, to help them bring their vision to life.

the brief

the project began with a thorough brief from the clients, where they expressed their desire for a modern, luxurious, and functional interior design. they wanted their home to reflect their personal style, with a mix of indian and western elements. the brief also included a specific timeline, with the project needing to be completed within 12 weeks. the total budget for the project was ₹35 lakh, which included all design, material, and labor costs.

initial design discussions

during the initial design discussions, our team at indéva studio presented the clients with a range of design options, including different layouts, materials, and color schemes. the clients were particularly drawn to a design that featured a mix of wood, metal, and glass elements, which they felt would give their home a luxurious and modern feel.

the challenge

one of the major challenges faced during the project was the limited budget. with ₹35 lakh, we had to be mindful of every design decision and ensure that it was within the budget. another challenge was the tight timeline, which meant that every aspect of the project had to be carefully planned and executed to ensure completion within the given timeframe.

overcoming budget constraints

to overcome the budget constraints, our team at indéva studio had to be creative with the design and material selection. we opted for cost-effective materials, such as engineered wood and metal, which not only fit within the budget but also added to the luxurious feel of the space. we also had to negotiate with contractors and suppliers to ensure that we were getting the best possible prices for the materials and labor.

the design solution

the final design solution included a mix of modern and traditional elements, with a focus on functionality and luxury. the living room featured a large wooden panel with a metal inlay, which added a touch of elegance to the space. the kitchen was designed with a sleek, modern look, featuring a mix of glass and metal elements. the bedrooms were designed to be cozy and intimate, with plush carpets and soft lighting.

  • living room: wooden panel with metal inlay, comfortable seating area, and a large window with a view of the surrounding landscape
  • kitchen: sleek, modern design with glass and metal elements, ample storage space, and a large cooking area
  • bedrooms: cozy and intimate, with plush carpets, soft lighting, and large windows with views of the surrounding landscape
the key to achieving affordable luxury interior design is to be mindful of every design decision and to opt for cost-effective materials and solutions without compromising on the quality and aesthetic of the space - indéva studio

the result

the final result was a stunning luxury interior design that exceeded the clients' expectations. the project was completed within the given timeline of 12 weeks and within the budget of ₹35 lakh. the clients were thrilled with the final outcome and felt that their dream of a luxury home had been fulfilled.

final cost vs budget

the final cost of the project was ₹32 lakh, which was within the budget of ₹35 lakh. the clients were pleased with the cost-effectiveness of the design and the fact that we were able to deliver a luxury interior design within their budget.
