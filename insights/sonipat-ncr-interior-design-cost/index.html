the surprising truth about interior design costs in sonipat ncr

many people in india think that hiring an interior designer is a luxury only the rich can afford, but the truth is, a good designer can actually help you save money in the long run by avoiding costly mistakes and ensuring that your ₹40–80 lakh budget is spent wisely, whether you're designing a luxury apartment in dlf phase 5, gurgaon, or a cozy home in sonipat ncr.

what drives costs up in interior design projects

several factors can drive up the cost of an interior design project, including the size of the space, the materials used, and the complexity of the design, for example, a luxury apartment with high-end finishes and custom furnishings can cost upwards of ₹80 lakh, while a more modest design can be achieved for ₹40 lakh or less.

breakdown of costs by room

here's a rough breakdown of what you might expect to pay for different rooms in a luxury apartment in dlf phase 5, gurgaon:

  • living room: ₹5–10 lakh
  • kitchen: ₹8–15 lakh
  • bedroom: ₹3–6 lakh
  • bathroom: ₹2–4 lakh

how to negotiate with your interior designer

according to the national institute of design, a good interior designer should be able to provide you with a detailed breakdown of the costs involved in your project, including materials, labor, and overheads, this will help you understand where your money is going and make informed decisions about your budget.

at indéva studio, we believe in transparency and open communication, which is why we provide our clients with a detailed cost estimate and regular updates throughout the design process, you can check out our portfolio to see examples of our work and our design process to learn more about how we work.

red flags that inflate bills

there are several red flags to watch out for when hiring an interior designer, including:

  • hidden fees or charges
  • low-quality materials or workmanship
  • poor communication or lack of transparency

real project budget example

for example, let's say you're designing a luxury apartment in dlf phase 5, gurgaon, with a budget of ₹60 lakh, here's how the costs might break down:

  • design and planning: ₹6 lakh
  • materials and furnishings: ₹30 lakh
  • labor and installation: ₹15 lakh
  • overheads and contingencies: ₹9 lakh
