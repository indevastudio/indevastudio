luxury home interior design cost delhi 2025: a warning guide

not planning for hidden costs

What goes wrong: hidden costs such as electrical and plumbing work can quickly add up, blowing your budget. Why it happens: many homeowners focus solely on the visible elements of design, neglecting the essential behind-the-scenes work. Exact fix: work with a designer who can help you plan for these costs from the outset, allocating a contingency fund of around ₹2-3 lakh.

choosing the wrong materials

What goes wrong: selecting materials that are not durable or suitable for your space can lead to costly replacements down the line. Why it happens: the allure of cheap or trendy materials can be tempting, but it's essential to prioritize quality and functionality. Exact fix: invest in high-quality materials that fit your budget, such as solid wood or engineered wood, which can cost between ₹50-200 per square foot.

tips for selecting the right materials

  • research different materials and their pros and cons
  • consider the lifestyle and usage of each room
  • don't compromise on quality, even if it means spending a bit more
as a designer, i always advise my clients to invest in materials that will stand the test of time, rather than opting for cheap alternatives that may need to be replaced soon, which can be a significant expense, often in the range of ₹1-2 lakh.

not considering the resale value

What goes wrong: designing a space that is too personalized or trendy can make it difficult to sell in the future. Why it happens: homeowners often focus on their current needs and tastes, neglecting the potential resale value of their property. Exact fix: work with a designer who can help you strike a balance between personalization and timeless design, which can increase the resale value of your property, potentially by ₹5-10 lakh.

not hiring a professional designer

What goes wrong: attempting to design a luxury home interior without professional help can lead to costly mistakes and a space that doesn't meet your needs. Why it happens: many homeowners underestimate the complexity of interior design, thinking they can handle it themselves. Exact fix: hire a professional designer who can guide you through the process, providing valuable expertise and connections to reliable contractors and suppliers, which can save you around ₹3-5 lakh in the long run.
