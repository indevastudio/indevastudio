modern villa design ideas for indian homes: a case study

in the heart of lutyens delhi, delhi, a family of four was living in a heritage bungalow that had been their home for generations. however, over time, the house had become outdated and no longer met their modern needs. they approached indéva studio with a budget of ₹8-15 lakh to redesign their home and create a modern villa that would preserve the historical charm of the bungalow while incorporating contemporary elements.

the brief

the client's brief was to create a modern villa design that would respect the heritage of the bungalow while meeting their current needs. they wanted to retain the original façade and structure of the house but make significant changes to the interior to create a more open and modern living space. the budget of ₹8-15 lakh was to cover all the redesign and renovation work, including new flooring, lighting, and furniture.

understanding the client's needs

our team at indéva studio spent several weeks understanding the client's needs and preferences. we conducted a thorough analysis of the site, taking into account the natural light, ventilation, and existing layout of the house. we also had several meetings with the client to discuss their lifestyle, hobbies, and expectations from the redesigned home.

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges we faced was balancing the modern villa design with the historical significance of the heritage bungalow. we had to ensure that the changes we made did not compromise the integrity of the original structure. additionally, we had to work within the given budget of ₹8-15 lakh, which meant making careful decisions about the materials and finishes we used.

preserving the historical charm

to preserve the historical charm of the bungalow, we decided to retain the original wooden doors and windows, which were restored and refinished to maintain their original character. we also kept the original flooring in some areas, such as the living room, and used it as a reference point for the rest of the house.

the design solution

our design solution was to create a modern villa that would respect the heritage of the bungalow while incorporating contemporary elements. we opened up the interior spaces to create a more open and modern living area, while retaining the original façade and structure of the house. we used a combination of traditional and modern materials, such as wood, stone, and steel, to create a unique and distinctive look.

  • we used reclaimed wood for the flooring and furniture to add warmth and character to the space
  • we installed new lighting fixtures, including pendant lights and table lamps, to create a modern and sleek look
  • we designed a custom kitchen with sleek cabinets and countertops, and a large center island for food preparation and entertainment
the key to creating a successful modern villa design is to strike a balance between traditional and contemporary elements. by respecting the heritage of the bungalow while incorporating modern materials and finishes, we were able to create a unique and distinctive space that met the client's needs and exceeded their expectations.

the result

the final result was a modern villa that respected the heritage of the bungalow while incorporating contemporary elements. the client was thrilled with the outcome, and the project was completed within the budget of ₹12 lakh. the redesigned home featured a more open and modern living space, with a combination of traditional and modern materials and finishes.
