how to negotiate with your interior designer

red flags that inflate bills

there are several red flags to watch out for when working with an interior designer, including:

  • hidden costs or fees
  • poor communication or lack of transparency
  • low-quality materials or workmanship
by being aware of these potential pitfalls, you can avoid unexpected expenses and ensure that your design project stays on track and within budget.
