step 1: conceptualization and planning

at this stage, you need to define your restaurant's concept, target audience, and budget. what to do: determine your restaurant's theme, cuisine, and target audience. what to ask your designer: what are the latest trends in restaurant interior design in gurgaon? common pitfall: not considering the space's limitations and potential.

step 2: space planning and layout

once you have a clear concept, you need to plan the space. what to do: create a floor plan and layout that maximizes the use of space. what to ask your designer: how can we optimize the seating capacity while maintaining a comfortable dining experience? common pitfall: not leaving enough space for circulation and services.

key considerations for space planning

  • seating capacity and layout
  • circulation and accessibility
  • services and utilities
  • acoustics and lighting

step 3: design development and visualization

with a clear concept and space plan, you can start developing the design. what to do: create a mood board and select materials, colors, and textures that reflect your restaurant's theme and brand. what to ask your designer: how can we use lighting and furniture to create a unique ambiance? common pitfall: not considering the budget and feasibility of the design.
