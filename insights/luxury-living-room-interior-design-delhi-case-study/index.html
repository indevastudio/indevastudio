luxury living room interior design delhi: a case study

our client, a successful business owner, had recently purchased a beautiful farmhouse in kundli, sonipat, with the intention of creating a luxurious retreat for his family. however, the living room, which was supposed to be the heart of the home, felt cold and uninviting. the client approached us with a budget of ₹1-3 crore to design a luxury living room that would reflect his personal style and provide a comfortable space for his family to relax and entertain.

the brief

the client's brief was to create a warm and inviting living room that would showcase his collection of artwork and provide ample seating for his family and friends. he wanted the space to feel luxurious, yet comfortable and relaxed. our team at indéva studio began by conducting a thorough analysis of the client's lifestyle, preferences, and needs. we also surveyed the site, taking note of the natural light, ventilation, and existing architectural features.

understanding the client's needs

through our discussions with the client, we gained a deep understanding of his vision and priorities. we learned that he valued comfort, elegance, and functionality, and that he wanted the living room to be a space where his family could come together and create lasting memories. we also identified the client's favorite colors, materials, and design styles, which would later inform our design decisions.

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges we faced was the large size of the living room, which measured over 1,000 square feet. the space felt overwhelming, and it was difficult to create a sense of intimacy and warmth. additionally, the client's collection of artwork was extensive, and we needed to find a way to showcase each piece in a way that would do it justice. our team worked closely with the client to develop a design concept that would address these challenges and create a cohesive, functional space.

developing a design concept

after careful consideration, we developed a design concept that would divide the living room into smaller, more intimate areas. we created a seating area with a comfortable sofa and armchairs, a reading nook with a floor lamp and a side table, and a display area for the client's artwork. we also incorporated natural materials, such as wood and stone, to add warmth and texture to the space.

  • seating area with a comfortable sofa and armchairs
  • reading nook with a floor lamp and a side table
  • display area for the client's artwork
  • incorporation of natural materials, such as wood and stone
the key to creating a sense of intimacy in a large space is to divide it into smaller, more manageable areas. by doing so, you can create a sense of warmth and coziness, even in a space that feels overwhelming.

the design solution

our design solution was to create a luxury living room that would feel warm, inviting, and elegant. we selected a palette of rich, earthy colors, including beige, brown, and green, which would complement the client's artwork and add warmth to the space. we also incorporated luxurious materials, such as marble and leather, to add depth and texture. the client's artwork was showcased throughout the space, with each piece carefully lit and displayed to maximize its impact.

selecting materials and finishes

we worked closely with the client to select materials and finishes that would reflect his personal style and meet his needs. we chose a combination of natural materials, such as wood and stone, and luxurious materials, such as marble and leather. the client's budget of ₹1-3 crore allowed us to specify high-quality materials and finishes, which would ensure that the space would look and feel luxurious for years to come.

the result

the final result was a stunning luxury living room that exceeded the client's expectations. the space felt warm, inviting, and elegant, with a perfect balance of comfort, functionality, and style. the client's artwork was showcased to perfection, and the natural materials and luxurious finishes added depth and texture to the space. the client was thrilled with the final result, and we were proud to have created a space that would be enjoyed by his family for years to come. the final cost of the project was ₹2.5 crore, which was within the client's budget.
