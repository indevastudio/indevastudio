farmhouse bedroom interior design india cost guide

may 2026 · 6 min read · indéva studio

the surprising truth about farmhouse bedroom interior design costs in india

when it comes to designing a luxury farmhouse bedroom in india, many people assume that the cost will be prohibitively expensive, with some estimates ranging as high as ₹1 lakh per square foot. however, with a budget of ₹25-50 lakh, it's possible to create a stunning and functional space that meets your needs and exceeds your expectations. according to a report by architectural digest india, the average cost of designing a luxury bedroom in india can range from ₹5-15 lakh, depending on the size and scope of the project.

what drives up costs in farmhouse bedroom interior design

one of the biggest factors that drives up costs in farmhouse bedroom interior design is the use of high-end materials and finishes. for example, using imported marble or granite for the flooring can add ₹2-3 lakh to the overall cost of the project. similarly, choosing high-end lighting fixtures or designer furniture can add another ₹1-2 lakh to the bill. however, with some careful planning and budgeting, it's possible to achieve a similar look and feel without breaking the bank.

cost-effective alternatives

for example, instead of using imported marble, you could opt for a more affordable alternative like indian marble or even ceramic tiles, which can cost as little as ₹500-1000 per square foot. similarly, instead of choosing designer furniture, you could opt for more affordable options like ikea or even custom-made pieces from local artisans, which can cost between ₹50,000-1 lakh.

cost breakdown by room

when it comes to designing a luxury farmhouse bedroom, the cost can vary greatly depending on the size and scope of the project. here is a rough breakdown of the costs involved in designing a typical farmhouse bedroom:

  • flooring: ₹1-2 lakh
  • walls and ceilings: ₹1-2 lakh
  • lighting: ₹50,000-1 lakh
  • furniture: ₹1-2 lakh
  • decor and accessories: ₹50,000-1 lakh

as you can see, the total cost of designing a luxury farmhouse bedroom can range from ₹5-10 lakh, depending on the size and scope of the project. however, with a budget of ₹25-50 lakh, it's possible to create a truly stunning and functional space that meets your needs and exceeds your expectations.

how to negotiate with contractors and suppliers

one of the most important things to keep in mind when negotiating with contractors and suppliers is to always get multiple quotes and compare prices. this can help you get a better sense of the market rate for the materials and labor involved in the project, and can also give you some leverage when negotiating the final price. for example, if you're planning to design a farmhouse bedroom in a villa on golf course road, gurgaon, you may want to get quotes from multiple contractors and suppliers to compare prices and find the best deal.

according to a recent survey, nearly 70% of homeowners in india report being able to negotiate a better price with their contractor or supplier by simply asking for a discount. so don't be afraid to speak up and ask for a better deal - it could end up saving you thousands of rupees in the long run.

red flags that inflate bills

when it comes to designing a luxury farmhouse bedroom, there are several red flags to watch out for that can inflate the bill and blow your budget. some common examples include:

  • hidden costs or fees: always make sure to get a detailed breakdown of the costs involved in the project, and ask about any potential hidden costs or fees.
  • low-quality materials: always inspect the materials and finishes used in the project to ensure they meet your standards and expectations.
  • poor workmanship: always check the workmanship and attention to detail in the project to ensure it meets your standards and expectations.

by being aware of these potential red flags and taking steps to mitigate them, you can help ensure that your luxury farmhouse bedroom design project stays on budget and meets your expectations.

real project budget example

for example, let's say you're planning to design a luxury farmhouse bedroom in a villa on golf course road, gurgaon, with a budget of ₹30 lakh. here's a rough breakdown of how you might allocate your budget:

  • flooring: ₹1.5 lakh
  • walls and ceilings: ₹1.5 lakh
  • lighting: ₹75,000
  • furniture: ₹1.5 lakh
  • decor and accessories: ₹75,000
