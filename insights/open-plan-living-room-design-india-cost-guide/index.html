open plan living room design india cost guide — cost & budget guide

july 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio
photo by alex Roosso on unsplash

the surprising truth about open plan living room design costs in india

when it comes to designing an open plan living room in india, many homeowners are under the misconception that it will be a cost-effective option. however, the reality is that open plan living room design can be quite expensive, with costs ranging from ₹25 lakh to ₹50 lakh for a plotted home in rai, sonipat. in this article, we will explore the biggest cost misconceptions in india and provide a definitive cost guide for open plan living room design.

what drives costs up in open plan living room design

there are several factors that drive up costs in open plan living room design, including the size of the room, the materials used, and the design elements. for example, using high-end materials such as marble or granite can increase costs significantly. additionally, design elements such as a large skylight or a statement light fixture can also drive up costs.

materials and their costs

here are some common materials used in open plan living room design and their approximate costs:

  • marble flooring: ₹200-₹500 per square foot
  • granite flooring: ₹150-₹300 per square foot
  • wooden flooring: ₹100-₹200 per square foot

cost breakdown by room

to get a better understanding of the costs involved in open plan living room design, let's break down the costs by room. for a plotted home in rai, sonipat, with a budget of ₹25-₹50 lakh, here is a rough estimate of the costs:

  • living room: ₹10-₹20 lakh
  • dining room: ₹5-₹10 lakh
  • kitchen: ₹10-₹20 lakh

tips for staying within budget

to stay within your budget, it's essential to prioritize your needs and make smart design decisions. for example, instead of using marble or granite flooring, you could opt for wooden flooring or ceramic tiles. additionally, consider using energy-efficient lighting and appliances to reduce your energy costs.

according to the indian green building council, using energy-efficient materials and design elements can reduce energy costs by up to 30%.

how to negotiate with contractors and designers

when working with contractors and designers, it's essential to negotiate the costs and ensure that you're getting the best value for your money. here are some tips for negotiating with contractors and designers:

  • get multiple quotes from different contractors and designers
  • be clear about your budget and expectations
  • ask for discounts or bundle deals

red flags that inflate bills

there are several red flags that can inflate your bills when it comes to open plan living room design. these include:

  • hidden costs or unforeseen expenses
  • low-quality materials or workmanship
  • design elements that are not functional or practical

real project budget example

let's take a look at a real project budget example for an open plan living room design in a plotted home in rai, sonipat. for a budget of ₹30 lakh, here is a rough breakdown of the costs:

  • design and planning: ₹3 lakh
  • materials and labor: ₹20 lakh
  • lighting and electrical: ₹2 lakh
  • plumbing and fixtures: ₹1 lakh
  • contingency fund: ₹4 lakh

frequently asked questions

what is the average cost of open plan living room design in india?

the average cost of open plan living room design in india can range from ₹25 lakh to ₹50 lakh, depending on the size of the room, materials used, and design elements.

how long does it take to complete an open plan living room design project?

the duration of an open plan living room design project can vary depending on the complexity of the design and the size of the room. on average, it can take anywhere from 6-12 weeks to complete.

what are some common mistakes to avoid in open plan living room design?

some common mistakes to avoid in open plan living room design include not considering the flow of traffic, not providing enough storage, and not using energy-efficient materials and design elements.

how can i stay within my budget for open plan living room design?

to stay within your budget, prioritize your needs, make smart design decisions, and consider using energy-efficient materials and design elements. additionally, be sure to negotiate with contractors and designers to get the best value for your money.
