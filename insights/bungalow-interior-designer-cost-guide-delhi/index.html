cost guide for hiring a bungalow interior designer in delhi

september 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

Most home owners believe that hiring a bungalow interior designer in Delhi will automatically cost more than ₹20 lakh, but recent market data shows the average spend for a well‑executed project stays between ₹8 lakh and ₹15 lakh.

what drives costs up

Understanding the cost drivers helps separate the essential from the optional. In Delhi, the biggest factors are:

  • Material selection: Natural stone, premium hardwood, and custom‑made fixtures carry a high price tag.
  • Scope of work: Full‑home renovations versus selective room makeovers create a wide cost spectrum.
  • Design complexity: Intricate patterns, bespoke furniture, and integrated lighting systems add labour hours.
  • Regulatory compliance: For new builds, approvals from bodies like the indian green building council can add fees.

When a client asks for “luxury finishes,” the designer’s recommendation often reflects both aesthetic goals and the price ceiling set by these drivers.

cost breakdown by room

Below is a typical allocation of the ₹8–15 lakh budget for a 2,500 sq ft bungalow. Figures are averages; actual spend will vary with taste and location.

living room – ₹2.0–3.5 lakh

  • Flooring (marble or engineered wood): ₹80,000–₹1,20,000
  • Custom sofa set and coffee table: ₹60,000–₹1,00,000
  • Lighting (recessed + pendant): ₹40,000–₹80,000
  • Wall treatments (paint or cladding): ₹30,000–₹60,000

kitchen – ₹1.5–2.5 lakh

  • Cabinetry (solid wood): ₹90,000–₹1,30,000
  • Countertops (granite/quartz): ₹50,000–₹80,000
  • Appliances (integrated): ₹40,000–₹70,000
  • Plumbing & electrical: ₹20,000–₹40,000

master bedroom – ₹1.2–2.0 lakh

  • Wardrobe (built‑in): ₹70,000–₹1,00,000
  • Bed frame & headboard: ₹40,000–₹70,000
  • Soft furnishings (curtains, rugs): ₹30,000–₹50,000
  • Lighting & accessories: ₹20,000–₹30,000

additional rooms (guest, study, bathrooms) – ₹1.0–2.0 lakh

The remaining budget is split among bathrooms, a study, and any ancillary spaces. Bathrooms often consume a larger share due to waterproofing and sanitary ware.

how to negotiate with a bungalow interior design company Delhi

Negotiation is not about haggling; it’s about aligning expectations with realistic cost structures. Follow these steps:

  1. Ask for a detailed quote: Breakdowns should list material cost, labour, design fees, and contingency.
  2. Set a clear ceiling: Communicate the ₹15 lakh maximum early, so the designer can prioritize.
  3. Leverage bulk purchasing: If multiple rooms use the same material, ask for volume discounts.
  4. Consider phased implementation: Complete high‑impact areas first, then move to secondary spaces.
  5. Request value engineering: Ask the designer to suggest lower‑cost alternatives that retain the visual intent.

Professional designers from indéva studio often provide a “cost‑control worksheet” that tracks every expense against the original budget, helping clients stay on track.

red flags that inflate bills

Even seasoned homeowners can fall prey to practices that push costs beyond the planned range. Watch out for:

“A designer who does not disclose a clear material markup is often covering hidden supplier commissions.” – industry insight
  • Unexplained “admin fees” that appear after the contract is signed.
  • Frequent change orders without prior cost impact analysis.
  • Use of “premium” labels for standard‑grade products.
  • Lack of a signed, itemised contract; verbal agreements are hard to enforce.

When a designer pushes for high‑end brands without offering comparable alternatives, it’s a signal to pause and request justification.

frequently asked questions

Q1: How early should I involve a designer?

Ideally at the concept stage, before architectural drawings are finalised. Early involvement avoids costly rework.

Q2: Are there hidden taxes on interior works?

GST of 18% applies to most interior services and material purchases. Ensure the quote is GST‑inclusive to avoid surprises.

Q3: Can I source my own materials to cut costs?

Yes, but the designer must approve them for compatibility with the overall plan. Some firms charge a sourcing fee if you bring in third‑party items.

Q4: What is a reasonable contingency amount?

Ten percent of the total estimated cost is standard in Delhi. It covers unexpected site conditions or minor design tweaks.
