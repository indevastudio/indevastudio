restaurant interior designer delhi ncr: a case study

in a bustling city like delhi, finding a unique dining experience can be a challenge. for one of our clients, the goal was to create a restaurant in a heritage bungalow in lutyens delhi, delhi, that would stand out from the crowd. with a budget of ₹8-15 lakh, we at indéva studio were tasked with bringing this vision to life.

the brief

the client approached us with a clear vision: to create a restaurant that would showcase the best of indian cuisine in a historic setting. the heritage bungalow, with its high ceilings and large windows, provided the perfect backdrop for this vision. our team at indéva studio spent several weeks discussing the project with the client, understanding their requirements, and defining the scope of work.

project timeline

the project kicked off in january and was scheduled to be completed within 12 weeks. our team worked closely with the client to ensure that every detail, from the design to the construction, was meticulously planned and executed.

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges we faced was working within the constraints of a heritage building. the bungalow's historic charm had to be preserved, while also incorporating modern amenities and design elements. our team had to think creatively to ensure that the restaurant's design was both functional and aesthetically pleasing.

design decisions

to address the challenge, we made several key design decisions. we chose to retain the original woodwork and flooring, while incorporating modern materials and textures to add depth and visual interest. we also opted for a neutral color palette, with accents of warm colors to create a cozy and inviting atmosphere.

  • retained original woodwork and flooring to preserve the bungalow's historic charm
  • incorporated modern materials and textures to add depth and visual interest
  • chose a neutral color palette with warm accents to create a cozy atmosphere

the design solution

our design solution was to create a unique dining space that blended traditional and modern elements. we designed a layout that maximized the use of natural light, while also providing intimate seating areas for guests. the restaurant's décor was carefully curated to reflect the client's vision, with a mix of vintage and contemporary pieces.

the key to designing a successful restaurant is to create an atmosphere that is both inviting and immersive. by combining traditional and modern elements, we were able to create a unique dining experience that would leave a lasting impression on guests.

the result

the final result was a restaurant that exceeded the client's expectations. the heritage bungalow was transformed into a stunning dining space, with a unique blend of traditional and modern elements. the project was completed within the scheduled 12 weeks, and the final cost came in at ₹12 lakh, well within the budget of ₹8-15 lakh.

project outcome

the client was thrilled with the final outcome, and the restaurant quickly became a popular destination in lutyens delhi, delhi. our team at indéva studio was proud to have been a part of this project, and we were happy to have had the opportunity to work with such a unique and historic building. you can view more of our projects on our portfolio page.
