high end cafe and restaurant design delhi: a case study

in the heart of lutyens delhi, a young entrepreneur, rohan, had a vision to open a luxury cafe in a heritage bungalow. with a budget of ₹8-15 lakh, he approached indéva studio to bring his dream to life. the bungalow, with its stunning architecture and beautiful gardens, was the perfect location for a high-end cafe.

the brief

rohan's brief to indéva studio was to design a luxury cafe that would reflect the heritage and elegance of the bungalow. he wanted the cafe to have a unique and sophisticated atmosphere, with a seating capacity of around 50 people. the cafe would serve a range of specialty teas, coffees, and snacks, and would also have a small library and lounge area.

understanding the client's vision

to understand rohan's vision, the indéva studio team spent several days discussing his ideas and preferences. they also conducted a site analysis to understand the bungalow's architecture and how it could be used to create a unique and functional cafe space. the team decided to allocate ₹10 lakh for the design and construction of the cafe, with ₹3 lakh for furniture and fixtures, and ₹2 lakh for lighting and electrical work.

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges faced by the indéva studio team was to preserve the heritage and elegance of the bungalow while creating a modern and functional cafe space. the team had to work closely with the architects and conservation experts to ensure that the design and construction of the cafe did not damage the bungalow's structure or compromise its historical significance.

overcoming design and construction challenges

to overcome the design and construction challenges, the indéva studio team decided to use a combination of traditional and modern materials and techniques. they used reclaimed wood and stone to create a warm and elegant atmosphere, while also incorporating modern lighting and electrical systems to ensure that the cafe was functional and efficient. the team also worked closely with local artisans and craftsmen to create custom furniture and fixtures that reflected the bungalow's heritage and elegance.

  • reclaimed wood and stone were used to create a warm and elegant atmosphere
  • modern lighting and electrical systems were incorporated to ensure functionality and efficiency
  • custom furniture and fixtures were created to reflect the bungalow's heritage and elegance
the key to creating a successful luxury cafe is to understand the client's vision and preferences, and to work closely with them to bring their dream to life. in this case, rohan's passion for heritage and elegance was the driving force behind the design and construction of the cafe - architectural digest india

the design solution

the indéva studio team decided to create a unique and sophisticated atmosphere in the cafe by using a combination of traditional and modern design elements. they designed a beautiful and elegant seating area, with custom furniture and fixtures that reflected the bungalow's heritage and elegance. the team also created a stunning library and lounge area, with a range of books and magazines that catered to rohan's love of literature and poetry.

design decisions and rationale

the indéva studio team made several key design decisions to create a unique and functional cafe space. they decided to use a neutral color palette, with accents of wood and stone, to create a warm and elegant atmosphere. they also incorporated plenty of natural light, through large windows and skylights, to create a bright and airy feel. the team worked closely with rohan to select a range of specialty teas, coffees, and snacks that would cater to his target market and reflect the cafe's heritage and elegance.

the result

the final result was a stunning and elegant cafe that reflected the heritage and elegance of the bungalow. the indéva studio team had successfully created a unique and sophisticated atmosphere, with a range of beautiful and functional design elements. the cafe was completed within the budget of ₹10 lakh, with a final cost of ₹9.5 lakh. rohan was thrilled with the result, and the cafe quickly became a popular destination for locals and tourists alike.
