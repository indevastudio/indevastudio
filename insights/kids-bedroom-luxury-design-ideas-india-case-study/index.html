kids bedroom luxury design ideas india: a case study

in a luxurious villa on golf course road, gurgaon, a family of four was looking to renovate their kids' bedroom. with two children, a boy and a girl, aged 8 and 10, the parents wanted a space that was not only functional but also reflected their kids' unique personalities. the family approached indéva studio with a budget of ₹25-50 lakh and a timeline of 12 weeks.

the brief

the client brief was to design a luxury kids bedroom that was both fun and sophisticated. the room had to accommodate two separate sleeping areas, a study zone, and ample storage for toys, books, and clothes. the parents also wanted to incorporate a few bespoke elements that would make the room truly special.

understanding the client's needs

  • creating a functional layout that met the needs of both children
  • designing bespoke furniture pieces that reflected the kids' personalities
  • incorporating ample storage to keep the room clutter-free

the challenge

one of the biggest challenges we faced was designing a room that catered to the different needs and preferences of both children. the boy was fond of science and technology, while the girl loved art and music. we had to find a way to balance their interests and create a space that was cohesive and harmonious.

as designers, we often have to navigate complex family dynamics and find creative solutions that meet everyone's needs. in this case, we decided to use a neutral color palette as a backdrop and add pops of color through furniture and accessories to reflect each child's personality.

the design solution

our design solution was to create a spacious and airy room with a functional layout that met the needs of both children. we designed two separate sleeping areas, each with its own bespoke bed and storage unit. the study zone was equipped with a custom-made desk and chair, and we incorporated ample storage for toys, books, and clothes.

we also added a few special touches, including a built-in reading nook with a comfortable cushion and a floor lamp, and a display wall for showcasing the kids' artwork and achievements. the room was painted a soothing shade of white, and we added pops of color through furniture and accessories.

budget breakdown

our initial budget was ₹25-50 lakh, and we were able to complete the project within this range. here's a rough breakdown of how we allocated our budget:

  • furniture and fixtures: ₹12 lakh
  • materials and finishes: ₹8 lakh
  • labor and installation: ₹5 lakh
  • design and consultation: ₹5 lakh

the result

the final result was a luxurious kids bedroom that exceeded our clients' expectations. the room was functional, sophisticated, and reflected the unique personalities of both children. the family was thrilled with the outcome, and we were happy to have created a space that would be enjoyed by the kids for years to come.

the total cost of the project was ₹38 lakh, which was within our initial budget range. we were able to complete the project within the 12-week timeline, and the family was able to move in and start enjoying their new space.
