luxury farmhouse design delhi ncr: a haven of elegance

Imagine waking up to the warm sunshine streaming through the wooden beams of your luxury farmhouse in South Delhi, Delhi, the sound of birds chirping in the distance, and the aroma of freshly brewed coffee wafting from the kitchen. The ideal version of this space is a perfect blend of traditional Indian aesthetics and modern luxury, with intricate stone carvings, wooden accents, and lush greenery surrounding the property.

traditional indian farmhouse design

This design direction is inspired by the rich cultural heritage of India, with elements such as intricate stone carvings, wooden pillars, and traditional roofing. The materials used include local stones like jaisalmer and katni, teak wood, and terracotta tiles. The cost range for this design is approximately ₹18-22 lakh, making it suitable for those who appreciate traditional Indian architecture and are willing to invest in high-quality materials.

modern luxury farmhouse design

For those who prefer a more contemporary look, modern luxury farmhouse design is the way to go. This style features clean lines, minimal ornamentation, and an emphasis on functionality. Materials used include glass, steel, and concrete, with a cost range of ₹15-20 lakh. This design suits individuals who value simplicity and modernity, and are looking for a sleek and sophisticated living space.

key elements of modern luxury farmhouse design

  • large windows and sliding glass doors
  • minimalist interior decor
  • state-of-the-art home automation systems
the key to achieving a modern luxury look is to strike a balance between functionality and aesthetics, without compromising on comfort and convenience.

rural chic farmhouse design

This design direction is perfect for those who want to escape the hustle and bustle of city life and connect with nature. Rural chic farmhouse design features elements such as exposed brick walls, wooden beams, and vintage decor. The materials used include reclaimed wood, brick, and stone, with a cost range of ₹20-25 lakh. This design suits individuals who appreciate the charm of rural living and are looking for a unique and eclectic living space.

fusion farmhouse design

For those who want to combine different styles and create a unique look, fusion farmhouse design is the way to go. This style blends traditional Indian elements with modern materials and techniques, resulting in a truly one-of-a-kind living space. The cost range for this design is approximately ₹18-22 lakh, making it suitable for those who are looking for a bespoke design that reflects their individuality.
