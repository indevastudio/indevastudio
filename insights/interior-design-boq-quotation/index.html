Understanding Interior BOQ vs Quotation

The most common misconception about interior BOQ (Bill of Quantities) and quotation is that they are interchangeable terms, but in reality, they serve distinct purposes in the interior design process. For a home in Delhi NCR with an illustrative budget of ₹25–50 lakh, understanding the difference between the two is crucial for making informed decisions.

Material Properties

When comparing interior BOQ and quotation, it's essential to consider the material properties of the items listed. For instance, the cost of materials like wood, steel, and glass can vary significantly, and their properties can affect the overall durability and maintenance of the interior design. In Delhi NCR, the climate can be quite harsh, so it's essential to choose materials that can withstand the weather conditions.

Cost Difference

The cost difference between interior BOQ and quotation can be substantial, especially when it comes to high-end materials. For example, a wooden floor with a ₹30 lakh budget can be achieved with different types of wood, each with its own cost and durability characteristics. At indéva studio, we specify materials based on the client's budget and preferences, ensuring that the final result meets their expectations.

Material Boq

A material BOQ is a detailed list of materials required for a project, including their quantities and costs. This document helps clients understand the material requirements and costs involved in the project. For a ₹25–50 lakh budget, it's essential to have a clear understanding of the material BOQ to avoid cost overruns.

Durability and Maintenance

When comparing interior BOQ and quotation, it's essential to consider the durability and maintenance requirements of the materials listed. For instance, wooden furniture may require more maintenance than steel or glass. In Delhi NCR, the humidity and temperature fluctuations can affect the durability of materials, so it's crucial to choose materials that can withstand these conditions.

Moisture and Termite Behaviour

Moisture and termite behaviour are critical factors to consider when choosing materials for interior design in Delhi NCR. Some materials, like wood, are more prone to termite damage and moisture issues, while others, like steel and glass, are more resistant. At indéva studio, we consider these factors when specifying materials for our clients' projects.

Where Each Option Makes Sense

Interior BOQ and quotation each have their own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice between them depends on the specific project requirements. For a ₹25–50 lakh budget, a detailed BOQ can help clients understand the material requirements and costs involved, while a quotation can provide a more comprehensive overview of the project costs.
