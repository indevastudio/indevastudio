kids bedroom luxury design ideas india — cost & budget guide

june 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

the surprising truth about kids bedroom luxury design costs in india

when it comes to designing a luxury kids bedroom in india, many parents assume that the cost will be prohibitively expensive, with some estimates ranging as high as ₹50 lakh or more. however, at indéva studio, we've found that with careful planning and design, it's possible to create a stunning and functional kids bedroom within a budget of ₹8-15 lakh, even in upscale areas like lutyens delhi, delhi.

what drives costs up in kids bedroom design

there are several factors that can drive up the cost of a kids bedroom design project, including the use of high-end materials, custom furniture, and intricate design elements. for example, a single piece of custom furniture can cost anywhere from ₹50,000 to ₹2 lakh or more, depending on the design and materials used. additionally, the cost of labor in delhi can range from ₹500 to ₹2,000 per day, depending on the skill level and experience of the worker.

cost breakdown by room

to give you a better idea of what to expect, here's a rough breakdown of the costs involved in designing a luxury kids bedroom in india:

  • furniture: ₹1.5-3 lakh
  • lighting: ₹50,000-1 lakh
  • flooring: ₹1-2 lakh
  • walls and paint: ₹50,000-1 lakh
  • decor and accessories: ₹50,000-1 lakh
total cost: ₹4-8 lakh

how to save money without sacrificing style

one way to save money on your kids bedroom design project is to opt for more affordable materials and design elements. for example, instead of using expensive hardwood flooring, you could choose a more affordable option like engineered wood or laminate flooring. as architectural digest india notes, "the key to designing a beautiful and functional kids bedroom is to strike a balance between style and practicality."

by prioritizing your needs and wants, and being mindful of your budget, you can create a kids bedroom that is both luxurious and functional, without breaking the bank.

how to negotiate with contractors and designers

when working with contractors and designers, it's essential to negotiate the terms of your contract to ensure that you're getting the best possible price. here are a few tips to keep in mind:

  • get multiple quotes from different contractors and designers to compare prices
  • be clear about your budget and what you're willing to pay
  • don't be afraid to walk away if the price isn't right
by being informed and prepared, you can negotiate a fair price for your kids bedroom design project.

red flags that inflate bills

there are several red flags to watch out for when designing a kids bedroom, as they can quickly inflate your bill. these include:

  • unexpected changes to the design or scope of the project
  • low-quality materials or workmanship
  • poor communication or lack of transparency from the contractor or designer
by being aware of these potential pitfalls, you can avoid costly surprises and stay within your budget.
