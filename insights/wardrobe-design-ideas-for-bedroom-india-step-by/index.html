wardrobe design ideas for bedroom india

most people approach wardrobe design backwards, starting with the style or material they like, rather than considering their actual needs and space. here is the right sequence to follow for a successful wardrobe design project.

step 1: assess your needs and space

before you start designing your wardrobe, you need to assess your needs and space. what do you plan to store in your wardrobe? do you have a lot of clothes, shoes, or accessories? what is the size of your bedroom and where will the wardrobe be located? what is your budget for the project? for a budget of ₹15-25 lakh, you can get a high-quality wardrobe with advanced features.

what to ask your designer

when you meet with your designer, ask them to help you create a list of your storage needs and to assess your space. they can help you determine the best location for your wardrobe and the optimal size and layout.

common pitfall

a common pitfall is to design a wardrobe that is too large or too small for the space. this can lead to a wardrobe that is impractical or overwhelming. make sure to work with your designer to create a design that is proportional to your space and meets your needs.

step 2: choose your materials and style

once you have assessed your needs and space, you can start thinking about the materials and style of your wardrobe. what type of wood or material do you like? do you prefer a modern or traditional style? what color and finish do you want? for a budget of ₹15-25 lakh, you can choose from a wide range of materials, including solid wood, engineered wood, and laminates.

what to ask your designer

ask your designer to show you samples of different materials and styles. they can help you choose the best option for your budget and preferences.

common pitfall

a common pitfall is to choose a material or style that is not durable or practical. make sure to consider the maintenance and upkeep of your wardrobe when making your selection.

step 3: plan the layout and features

with your materials and style chosen, you can start planning the layout and features of your wardrobe. what type of shelves and drawers do you need? do you want a hanging rod or a shoe rack? what about lighting and ventilation? for a budget of ₹15-25 lakh, you can include advanced features such as soft-close drawers and LED lighting.

what to ask your designer

ask your designer to create a detailed layout and design for your wardrobe. they can help you choose the best features and layout for your needs and space.

common pitfall

a common pitfall is to overlook the importance of lighting and ventilation in your wardrobe. make sure to include these features in your design to keep your clothes and shoes fresh and dry.

step 4: select a designer and installer

once you have planned your wardrobe, you need to select a designer and installer. look for a company with experience in wardrobe design and installation, such as indéva studio. check their portfolio and reviews to ensure they can deliver high-quality work. you can check our portfolio at our portfolio to see our previous work.

what to ask your designer

ask your designer about their experience and process. what is their design philosophy? how will they ensure that your wardrobe is installed correctly and meets your needs?

common pitfall

a common pitfall is to choose a designer or installer who is not experienced or reputable. make sure to do your research and choose a company that can deliver high-quality work.

step 5: install and enjoy your new wardrobe

finally, it's time to install your new wardrobe. make sure to work with your designer and installer to ensure that the installation is done correctly and meets your needs. with a budget of ₹15-25 lakh, you can expect a high-quality installation that will last for years to come. you can get in touch with our designers at get in touch with our designers to discuss your project.

what to ask your designer

ask your designer about the installation process and what to expect. what is the timeline for the installation? what kind of maintenance and upkeep will be required?

a common pitfall is to not plan for maintenance and upkeep. make sure to ask your designer about the maintenance requirements for your wardrobe and plan accordingly. you can also check out architectural digest india for more tips and ideas on wardrobe design and maintenance.
