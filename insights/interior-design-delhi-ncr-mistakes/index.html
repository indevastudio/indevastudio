avoiding costly mistakes in interior design services delhi ncr reviews

The day a client called us mid-project, frantic about the delays and cost overruns on their penthouse in Sector 150, Noida, was a wake-up call for us at indéva studio. The client had allocated a budget of ₹2 crore for the project, but it was already overshooting by ₹50 lakh, and the timeline had been extended by six months. As we dug deeper, we realized that the client had made some critical mistakes in the planning phase, which had cascaded into a series of problems that were now affecting the entire project.

not defining the project scope clearly

One of the primary reasons for the project's failure was the lack of a clear scope definition. The client had not specified their requirements, and the design team had not pushed back to clarify the expectations. As a result, the project had grown in scope, and the costs had spiralled out of control. To avoid this mistake, it is essential to define the project scope clearly, including the deliverables, timelines, and budget.

why it happens

This mistake happens when clients are not experienced in managing large-scale interior design projects. They may not be aware of the importance of defining the scope or may not know how to communicate their requirements effectively.

exact fix

To fix this, it is crucial to have a detailed discussion with the client to understand their requirements and expectations. At indéva studio, we use a comprehensive questionnaire to gather information about the client's lifestyle, preferences, and goals. We also provide them with a detailed scope statement that outlines the deliverables, timelines, and budget.

  • define the project scope clearly
  • establish a detailed scope statement
  • communicate effectively with the client

not selecting the right materials and finishes

Another critical mistake that clients make is not selecting the right materials and finishes for their project. This can lead to a series of problems, including quality issues, maintenance problems, and even safety hazards. For example, using low-quality flooring materials can lead to scratches and dents, while using the wrong type of paint can result in uneven finishes.

when it comes to materials and finishes, it's not just about aesthetics; it's also about functionality and sustainability. At indéva studio, we believe in using materials that are not only beautiful but also durable and environmentally friendly.

not considering sustainability and energy efficiency

With the increasing focus on sustainability and energy efficiency, it is essential to consider these factors when designing a penthouse in Sector 150, Noida. At indéva studio, we believe in designing spaces that are not only luxurious but also sustainable. We work closely with clients to understand their lifestyle and preferences and design spaces that minimize energy consumption and reduce waste. We also recommend using materials and products that are certified by organizations such as the indian green building council.

not planning for hidden costs

Hidden costs can quickly add up and blow a project's budget. At indéva studio, we recommend planning for contingencies and hidden costs, such as unexpected site costs, changes to the design, and upgrades to materials and finishes. We also provide clients with a detailed breakdown of the costs, including the ₹1 lakh to ₹3 lakh that can be allocated for contingencies.

not working with experienced designers

Finally, not working with experienced designers can be a costly mistake. At indéva studio, we have a team of experienced designers who have worked on numerous high-end projects in Delhi NCR. We understand the local market, the materials, and the labour costs, and we can provide clients with accurate estimates and timelines. We also offer our services that include design, procurement, and project management, to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.
