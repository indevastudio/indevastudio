The parents' bedroom

Comfort without excess: a quiet, sophisticated room for the parents, resolved almost entirely through joinery and lighting.

Design intent

The room was developed through a design-intent presentation, “Modern Indian Heritage”: walnut and cane, sage and terracotta, brass and marble, with arches and crafted joinery for the TV unit, wardrobe, dresser, daybed and doors.

Joinery, drawn before it was built

Every joinery element was detailed before site work: the headboard wall with a mural flanked by cane detailing and brass sconces, an arched upholstered headboard, a cane-front TV console beside a cushioned window seat, and an olive fluted TV panel with a marble inlay and display shelving. The palette draws on walnut fluting, warm beige lacquer and brushed brass.

Layered lighting

Lighting was layered rather than applied: cove, panel and pendant sources — including brass pendants either side of the bed — let the room move from morning clarity to evening warmth.

What Indéva delivered

Interior design, custom furniture and joinery, and site execution over two months.

Living and dining

A contemporary Indian living room shaped by warmth, craft and quiet geometry. Living and dining stay connected along one axis, separated by a slatted teak screen rather than a wall, with the mandir set into the entry threshold. Colour is carried by material, not paint: walnut, muted olive, terracotta and antique brass.

Kitchen

Warm modernism in natural materials. Walnut-tone laminate carries the room, black stone and charcoal draw the edges, and a light marble-look splash lifts the eye. One continuous 3,302 mm composition: solid shutters, two lit black-framed niches and open shelves either side of the chimney.

Powder washroom

A contemporary interior with a quiet heritage note: warm greige stone, walnut and brass. The WC and basin share one continuous marble plane, a scalloped arch frames the mirror on the basin axis, and the plan stays exactly where the existing CAD places it.

Design DevelopmentDesign intent for client review. Not built yet.

Modern bedroom: the Golden Walnut and Charcoal Retreat

A modern bedroom planned in four zones: sleep and rest at the centred bed wall, dressing by the window light, media on a backlit TV wall, and reading on a window seat with storage below. Walnut fluting, warm beige lacquer, Calacatta marble and brushed brass, with 3000K cove and panel lighting.
