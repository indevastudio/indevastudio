Planning an apartment interior in Noida

  1. Check the society rules firstIf your society has rules on working hours, material movement or deposits, share them early so the site plan accounts for them.
  2. Decide what staysBuilder-supplied flooring, kitchens and wardrobes are sometimes worth keeping. We review them with you before designing around or replacing them.
  3. Fix the budget before finishesWe agree a budget range at the alignment stage and develop an itemised BOQ, so material choices are made against real numbers.

How much does interior design cost in Noida?

It depends on area, scope and finish level. We discuss budget openly before design starts and prepare an itemised BOQ rather than quoting a flat rate.

What information do you need for an initial estimate?

Property type, approximate area, rooms or spaces in scope, expected start date and a budget range if you have one. A floor plan helps.
