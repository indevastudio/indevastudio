Designing a DDA flat: what we plan for

  • Small, fixed rooms. DDA rooms leave little slack — a 1 BHK bedroom is 8'10" × 11'1" — so wardrobes, beds and TV units are sized to the plan, not picked from a catalogue.
  • Builder finishes stay. DDA hands over flooring, tiled bathrooms and fittings. We work with them, so you don't pay twice.
  • Walls and wiring. Many new DDA pockets in Narela are built with precast and prefab structural walls. We route new wiring through the ceiling and existing conduits rather than cutting into structural walls.
  • Galley kitchens and utility balconies. Kitchens are tight, so we use full-height storage, lofts and pull-outs, and move the washing machine to the utility balcony where there is one.
  • Lift and society rules. Material movement, debris and working hours follow the pocket's rules; we coordinate with the guards and RWA.
