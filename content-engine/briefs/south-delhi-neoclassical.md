## the one idea

neoclassical interior design works in a compact apartment only when the detailing is architectural and the furniture is not: mouldings and panelling on the walls, streamlined pieces in front of them, storage built into the panelling.

- angle: someone who loves the classical look but lives in an apartment — what to keep, what to leave out, how to stop it becoming ornate
