understanding custom furniture construction

august 2026 · 4 min read · indéva studio

how custom furniture is made

When it comes to custom furniture, many people assume that it's all about the design and aesthetic appeal. However, the reality is that the construction process plays a much bigger role in determining the quality and durability of the final product. In this article, we'll explore the technical aspects of custom furniture construction, focusing on the materials and techniques used in the process.

material properties

When it comes to custom furniture, the choice of material is crucial. Two popular options are solid wood and engineered wood. Solid wood is a popular choice for its natural beauty and durability, but it can be expensive, with prices ranging from ₹8 to ₹15 lakh for a single piece of furniture. Engineered wood, on the other hand, is more affordable and can be just as durable, but it may not have the same natural look and feel as solid wood.

cost difference

The cost difference between solid wood and engineered wood can be significant. For example, a solid wood dining table can cost upwards of ₹10 lakh, while an engineered wood table of similar quality can cost around ₹5 lakh. However, it's worth noting that the cost of custom furniture is not just about the material, but also the design, craftsmanship, and attention to detail that goes into each piece.

illustrative budget

For a home in Delhi NCR, with a budget of ₹8–15 lakh, it's possible to create a custom furniture piece that meets your needs and fits your style. For example, you could opt for a solid wood bed frame with an engineered wood headboard, or a dining table made from a combination of solid and engineered wood.

durability and maintenance

When it comes to durability and maintenance, solid wood and engineered wood have different requirements. Solid wood is prone to warping and cracking if not properly maintained, while engineered wood is more resistant to moisture and termite damage. However, both materials require regular polishing and upkeep to maintain their appearance and extend their lifespan.

  • Solid wood: requires regular polishing, prone to warping and cracking
  • Engineered wood: more resistant to moisture and termite damage, requires less maintenance
As a general rule, the more expensive the material, the more durable it will be. However, this doesn't always mean that the most expensive option is the best choice. It's about finding the right balance between quality, price, and aesthetics.

where each option makes sense

Solid wood is a good choice for pieces that will be used frequently, such as a dining table or bed frame. Engineered wood, on the other hand, is better suited for pieces that will be exposed to moisture or termite damage, such as a kitchen cabinet or outdoor furniture. At indéva studio, we specify solid wood by default for most of our custom furniture projects, but we also offer engineered wood as an option for clients who are looking for a more affordable solution.

frequently asked questions

What is the difference between solid wood and engineered wood?

Solid wood is made from a single piece of wood, while engineered wood is made from multiple layers of wood that are pressed together.

How do I maintain my custom furniture?

Regular polishing and upkeep are necessary to maintain the appearance and extend the lifespan of your custom furniture. The specific maintenance requirements will depend on the material and design of the piece.
