About Indéva Studio · Luxury Gurgaon

Luxury, defined by
restraint.

We treat luxury as a material and spatial discipline. For our Gurgaon clients — homeowners in DLF's premium towers and Golf Course Road villas — that means a smaller, more confident material palette, joinery built to last, and lighting planned as a first-order decision rather than an afterthought.

We manage society NOC and RWA approvals as a standard part of every DLF project, since that process is often the real bottleneck on a premium high-rise timeline, not the design or fabrication work itself.
