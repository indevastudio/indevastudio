About This Guide · South Delhi

Why interior design pricing
varies so much.

Interior design cost in South Delhi varies more than in a typical high-rise market, because the housing stock itself varies more — a 2,000 sq ft builder floor, an 8,000 sq ft bungalow and a Sainik Farms farmhouse are genuinely different cost problems, not the same project scaled up. The ranges below are indicative starting points, not fixed prices.

At Indéva Studio, every quote is itemised — design fees, furniture, materials, and execution/labour are broken out separately, so you can see exactly what you're paying for and where cost-saving trade-offs are possible, rather than a single lump-sum number.

What Affects Cost · South Delhi

What drives interior
design cost.

Six factors that move the number most, independent of the property type table below.

01
Material Grade
The single biggest cost lever. Mid-range laminate and veneer work costs a fraction of natural stone, solid wood and imported finishes — and the difference compounds across a full property.
02
Extent of Custom Furniture
Fully custom, built-in furniture costs more upfront than modular or semi-custom pieces, but is specified to fit the space exactly and last longer.
03
Structural or MEP Correction
Older South Delhi bungalows and builder floors often need electrical, plumbing or structural correction alongside interior work — a cost driver apartments in newer towers rarely have.
04
Built-Up Area & Number of Floors
Multi-floor villas cost more per sq ft to coordinate than a single-level apartment, due to vertical circulation and duplicated services.
05
Design Complexity
Custom joinery, curved surfaces, integrated lighting and bespoke detailing all add design and fabrication time beyond a standard fit-out.
06
Timeline Compression
Faster timelines usually mean more parallel trades on site and premium vendor scheduling, which adds cost compared to a standard sequenced execution plan.
South Delhi Cost Context

How South Delhi differs
from other markets.

Unlike Gurgaon or Noida, where most of our enquiries are for apartments in newer towers, South Delhi work skews toward independent houses, builder floors and full-plot properties — many built decades ago. That means South Delhi projects more often carry a structural or MEP correction cost that a newer high-rise apartment simply doesn't have, and it's worth budgeting for that possibility during the initial site assessment rather than discovering it mid-project.
