featured case study
Hospitality · Murthal, Haryana · 23 Guest Rooms

hotel silk by resham

rooms
23
duration
12 months
handed over
30 Aug 2026

the brief

Murthal is where Delhi stops to eat. The owners wanted guests to stay — so a food-led destination needed 23 rooms worth sleeping in, built within a fixed budget.

the challenge

The rooms sat on the third floor of a busy building. Every duct, pipe and fitting had to be carried up, fitted precisely and built to last — for guests who would use them hard, every single day.

the indéva approach

So we designed for the long run: centralised VRV air-conditioning instead of split units, double-glazed windows to shut out the highway, soft ambient lighting that keeps power bills low, and fittings chosen so nothing needs constant repair.

PUF furniture MDF panelling Textured finishes Wooden flooring Italian marble Decorative lighting

the outcome

Today, 23 rooms that feel premium the moment you walk in — and keep working long after check-out. Comfortable for guests, easy for the owners to run.

Designed with intent. Engineered for everyday use.

featured case study · gurgaon

casa bianca

Private Farmhouse · 10,000 sq. ft. · Neoclassical Architecture

An expression of timeless elegance, designed around the art of living.

10,000sq. ft. carpet area
~10bedrooms
45°Cexecution conditions
8shaped swimming pool
the vision

They called it the White House. The family wanted a private retreat with real grandeur — around ten bedrooms, a basement for play, an eight-shaped pool on a granite deck, gardens, wide balconies and a guard room at the gate.

the challenge

The site was remote and the summer unforgiving: 45°C on site, frequent power cuts, scarce supplies and costly transport. Below ground, high and saline groundwater pressed against the basement — and the budget was fixed.

the outcome

A house that feels grand without forgetting it is a home. From the precision-cut pool deck to the dry basement beneath, every detail was built to look good and keep working.

the indéva approach
ArchitectureA Vastu-compliant plan with neoclassical detailing and rooms that breathe naturally.
WaterproofingBasement, bathrooms, balconies and terrace sealed against groundwater for the long run.
Pool & landscapeAn eight-shaped pool, its granite coping cut by hand to follow every curve.
InteriorsPremium floors, UPVC windows, crafted POP ceilings — and one statement chandelier.
KitchenAn HDHMR laminate kitchen with a breakfast counter and a pantry behind rolling shutters.
MEPElectrical, plumbing and drainage planned so the house simply works, every day.

10,000 sq. ft. of considered design. One vision, executed down to the last detail.

the dholpur stone villa

Heritage Restoration · Residential Renovation · Narela, Delhi
35+years of architectural heritage
15–20layout iterations
1,000sq. ft. new wall construction
500sq. ft. walls demolished
  1. 01
    the storyThirty-five years ago, Rajasthani craftsmen built this villa by hand, stone by stone. The family wanted it ready for modern life — without losing what made it special.
  2. 02
    the challengeIts walls carry the house, so even small changes needed an engineer on site. The old plan ignored Vastu, and it took 15–20 layout iterations to balance family, Vastu and structure.
  3. 03
    the outcomeThree decades of craftsmanship, kept. A home being rebuilt for the way the family lives today.
the indéva approach
StructureLoad-bearing walls modified under engineer supervision, reinforced where needed.
LayoutA Vastu-oriented plan with attached bathrooms and walk-in wardrobes.
FaçadeThe original Dholpur stone restored with a mineral coating — never paint.
Kitchen & stairA contemporary kitchen with breakfast counter and a redesigned staircase.
InteriorsNeoclassical detailing, with the original marble kept wherever possible.
ServicesNew concealed electrical and plumbing, waterproofing and ambient lighting.

Preserving its heritage. Rebuilding its future.
