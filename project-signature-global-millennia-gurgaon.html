The brief

Two bedrooms in the same apartment that should not feel like copies of each other. The master bedroom needed generous storage and a calm, hotel-like finish; the guest bedroom needed to feel fresh and welcoming, with display space and a little more colour.

Design intent

Each room was developed through a design-intent set of four views: the bed wall, the wardrobe, the window and the TV wall. Panelling, joinery, lighting and soft furnishings are resolved together, so every wall of the room is planned rather than left to chance.

Master bedroom: walnut, taupe and smoked glass

Walnut wainscot runs at headboard height with taupe panels above, finished with a fluted ivory panel and a brass wall light beside the upholstered bed. A floor-to-ceiling wardrobe with smoked-glass shutters in slim black frames sits beside open display shelves lit by concealed LED. Opposite, the TV wall pairs taupe panels and bronze strips with fluted edges over a floating walnut console, under a curved cove ceiling with profile light.

Guest bedroom: sage-teal and cane

A sage-teal panelled wall with fine vertical grooves sets the tone behind a beige upholstered bed and teal-and-cane side tables. The full-height teal wardrobe has cane shutters and curved open corner shelves, the TV wall uses grey textured panels with dark groove lines, and a rounded teal shelf unit adds display space. A tray ceiling with cove light and downlights keeps the room soft in the evening.
