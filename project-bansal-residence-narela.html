Bansal Residence, Narela

A ground-floor home being replanned around Vastu and the way the family lives day to day, while changing as little of the existing structure as possible.

01

The brief

The family's requirements, as agreed at the start of design:

  • Mandir in the north-east
  • Kitchen in the south-east
  • A bedroom on the ground floor, which is mandatory
  • A new washroom with both Western and Indian-style seating
  • No dining table
  • A guest lounge, diwan or multipurpose sitting area
  • The rear shaft, possibly used as a dry pantry
  • Minimum structural changes
02

The planning problem

Every item on that list fixes a room to a direction or a size. Vastu places the mandir and kitchen; the family's routine needs a ground-floor bedroom and a washroom that works for every generation; and the structure should stay largely as it is. The work in this stage is finding the one layout where all of those agree.

03

Our approach

  • Plan before finishes: layout options are tested against the brief and the existing walls before any material is chosen.
  • Use what exists: the rear shaft is being studied as a dry pantry rather than left as dead space.
  • Flexible sitting: with no dining table, a diwan or multipurpose lounge takes on guests, meals and everyday sitting.
  • Washroom for everyone: both Western and Indian-style seating planned in one new washroom.
